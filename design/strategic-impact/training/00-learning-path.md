# Strategic Impact — Learning Path

Reads before starting: `../functional-analysis.md`, `../standard/1-business-acumen-strategy.md` (the one PF), `../assessment/badges.md` (the 6-badge catalog, SI-1-P2 through SI-1-P7), the three instrument files in `../assessment/pf1-business-acumen-strategy/`, `../../industry-evidence/strategic-impact.md`, `../../COMPANY-CONTEXT.md`.

**This run rebuilds `training/` end-to-end against the redesigned badge system.** Not level-scoped — all six badges (P2–P7) get a training unit, matching the standard's own P2–P7 range. What differs by level is the *shape* of the unit: P2–P4 get guided exercises building rubric/checklist evidence directly; P5–P7 get autonomous, real-work-anchored projects, because — per the standard's own Evidence Guide and the badge catalog's tier definitions — facilitation/multiplier/steward-scope behavior cannot be manufactured in a sandboxed exercise. See `05-badge-reference-card.md` for the full badge-to-training-unit map.

## Prerequisites checklist

Before starting this learning path, confirm:

- [ ] Currently assigned to a client account/team long enough to describe what that team's domain actually does and what LFT delivers for it — every piece of evidence in this competency's badges is anchored to a real client engagement, not a generic exercise
- [ ] Has read `../../lft-engineering-competency-matrix.md` Section 5 "Strategic Impact," so the P2–P7 language isn't unfamiliar
- [ ] Has access to `../../my-role.md` and `../../goals/company-okr-2026.md` — the concrete artifacts this competency's "engineering strategy" language traces to; without reading these, "explain the organization's engineering strategy" has nothing real to point to
- [ ] Knows their own engagement's contract type (T&M or fixed-bid) — ask a manager or delivery lead if unsure; this is required from P3 up and cannot be looked up from a public source
- [ ] Delivery's PF 1 (Incremental Value Delivery) is a helpful but not required prerequisite — its P5+ "priority-setting aligned with organizational strategy" language overlaps in spirit with this competency
- [ ] Has read `../assessment/badges.md` §1.5–§1.8 (evidence-extraction, access-loss, assignment-limited, and services-signal handling) — nearly every badge in this catalog routes through one of these at some point, and knowing they exist before you need them prevents a stalled-evidence problem from feeling like a failed badge

Autonomous Projects are real assigned work on a real timeline, not sandboxed exercises — Project 3 in particular depends on a real strategic or growth opportunity existing, which will not always be available on demand; see the P5→P6 and P6→P7 gates below.

**Levels built out this run:** all six (P2–P7) — this run is not level-scoped, matching the standard's full P2–P7 range and the badge catalog's six badges. Nothing is deferred to a later run.

## Level gates

- **P2 → P3 (SI-1-P2 → SI-1-P3):** passes the work-product review rubric at "Meets P3" on Business/domain understanding and Product judgment, and holds the structured conversation (checklist) at the P3 bar unaided, including the T&M/fixed-bid item.
- **P3 → P4 (SI-1-P3 → SI-1-P4):** passes "Meets P4" on all three rubric rows (the decision, roadmap-feedback, and simplification items are non-substitutable per the badge catalog's completion bar), plus the co-signed confirmation that the decision's client/contract context is real. Completing Exercises 1–5 and Autonomous Project 1 is the on-ramp; the badge itself requires real, documented work, not the exercise output alone.
- **P4 → P5 (SI-1-P4 → SI-1-P5):** scope-of-role transition (execution → facilitation/advisory) — not producible by a guided exercise. Gate is the Phase D portfolio requirements for P5, using real on-the-job evidence. Autonomous Project 2 is the on-ramp, not a substitute for the portfolio review; the feature-evaluation item is non-substitutable *when a feature-evaluation opportunity existed during the review period* — if none arose, this routes through `badges.md` §1.7, not a failed gate.
- **P5 → P6 (SI-1-P5 → SI-1-P6):** same pattern, scope widens from one team/account to several teams and, distinctly for a services company, potentially several client accounts. Gate is the P6 portfolio requirements: a led cross-team strategic effort and an account-growth plan, both non-substitutable when the relevant opportunity existed in the period (§1.7 covers the case where it didn't). Autonomous Project 3's first half is the on-ramp.
- **P6 → P7 (SI-1-P6 → SI-1-P7):** gate is the P7 portfolio requirements: an org-wide strategic decision/plan led (non-substitutable), plus at least one of the three P7 sub-paths — cross-org roadmap, multi-account strategic influence, or the one-off high-stakes decision/client-executive-trust signal. Autonomous Project 3's second half is the on-ramp for the org-wide decision specifically; the "at least one of three" structure exists precisely because not every candidate's assignment will produce all three paths in one review cycle — see `badges.md` §1.7 if all three are blocked by assignment shape for more than one cycle.

There is no further guided/autonomous *content* at P5–P7 scale beyond the projects above — readiness from here is demonstrated through actual cross-team/cross-account/org-wide strategic work, assessed via portfolio + (at P5/P6) a collaboration/facilitation observation + (at P7) a strategy teaching/communication demonstration, per the standard's Evidence Guide.

## Skip guide

- **Experienced hire joining at P4 or above:** skip concept notes and guided exercises entirely. Start at Autonomous Project 1 or 2, and use `../assessment/pf1-business-acumen-strategy/03-portfolio-requirements.md` to identify what evidence to bring from a prior role. Note that prior-role evidence about a *different* client's contract economics does not automatically transfer — the P4 checklist item specifically asks about *this* engagement's contract type.
- **Experienced hire joining at P3:** skim concept notes §1–3 as a refresher, go straight to Exercises 2 and 4 to confirm baseline, then proceed to Project 1.
- **Anyone who has held a product-facing role before (PM, product-minded engineer):** skip Exercise 3's product-suggestion half — the roadmap-feedback habit will likely already be present — but still do the contract-economics half of Exercise 2 and all of Exercise 4, since LFT's specific services-company contract-economics content (T&M vs. fixed-bid, margin, utilization) will not transfer from a product-company background.
- **Anyone whose prior role included account/relationship management:** skip Exercise 5 (account-growth spotting) — that instinct usually transfers — but still build the documented artifact the exercise produces, since SI-1-P4's badge requires a *documented* instance, not just the underlying skill.
