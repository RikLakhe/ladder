# Strategic Impact — Autonomous Projects

Three open-ended projects, mapping to the whole PF (Business Acumen & Strategy) at increasing scope: P4 → P5 → P6/P7. No step-by-step guide — acceptance criteria only. Each project runs against **real assigned work**, not a sandboxed simulation — per the standard's own Evidence Guide, P5+ evidence is portfolio-based precisely because facilitation/multiplier/steward-scope behavior cannot be manufactured outside real work.

None of these projects award a badge by themselves. A badge still requires the named Verifier's (and, at P4/P6, co-signer's) sign-off per `../assessment/badges.md` §1.1–§1.3. See `05-badge-reference-card.md` for the full evidence-to-instrument map, and the assessment instruments themselves (`../assessment/pf1-business-acumen-strategy/`) for the actual rubric a "good" solution is measured against — this file states acceptance criteria only, not the full grading detail.

## Project 1 — Ship a contract-aware decision + roadmap-feedback + simplification bundle

**[P4 target]**

**Maps to sub-functions:** 1.3, 1.17, 1.18, 1.19, extends 1.20/1.23 for services context
**Builds toward badge:** SI-1-P4 (Contract-Aware Decision Driver)
**Rubric this is measured against:** `../assessment/pf1-business-acumen-strategy/01-work-product-review-rubric.md` Meets-P4 row, and `02-live-demo-checklist.md` P4 items

**Description:** Over your current assignment, assemble the full P4 evidence bundle from real work: one documented decision where contract economics or an adjacent team's domain genuinely changed your approach; one documented instance of roadmap feedback given to the project team; one documented instance of identifying a project/technical simplification opportunity; and, if a genuine opening arose, one documented account-growth or expansion opportunity you noticed and raised. This is the same bundle Guided Exercises 3–5 rehearse — this project is where you complete it against real, badge-eligible work rather than practice material.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- The decision, roadmap-feedback, and simplification items are each backed by a real, dated, retrievable artifact — not a description given verbally in the review conversation.
- The decision item specifically shows the business/contract factor changing the outcome, not just being mentioned afterward.
- The account-growth item is included if a genuine opportunity arose in the review period; if none did, this is stated honestly rather than manufactured — see `badges.md` §1.7.
- Your delivery/account manager has confirmed (in writing, as the required co-signer) that the client/contract context described in the decision item is real.
- If any artifact can't leave the client's environment, the in-place attestation path (`badges.md` §1.5) is used instead of omitting the item.

**What a good solution looks like:** see `01-work-product-review-rubric.md` Meets-P4 column and the "good" bar isn't separately defined at P2–P4 (rubric uses pass/does-not-meet) — the pass bar itself, applied with no HIGH gap on any of the three non-substitutable items, is the target.

## Project 2 — Trusted-advisor roadmap cycle

**[P5 target]**

**Maps to sub-functions:** 1.4, 1.9, 1.10, 1.20, 1.21, 1.22, extends 1.20/1.23 for services context
**Builds toward badge:** SI-1-P5 (Trusted Advisor — Roadmap & Economics)
**Rubric this is measured against:** `../assessment/pf1-business-acumen-strategy/03-portfolio-requirements.md` P5 rows, plus the P5/P6 collaboration/facilitation observation described in its "Review process" section

**Description:** Across one full roadmap or planning cycle (typically 4–8 weeks, following your account's actual cadence — don't compress this artificially), build a portfolio of P5-level evidence from real work: a decision connecting your domain, LFT/org strategy, and market trends, with adjacent-team strategy awareness; a team engineering-work decision made collaboratively and tied to org strategy; regular (multi-cycle, not one-off) roadmap participation with at least one proactive simplification example; and, if the assignment provides the opening, a client feature evaluated or created in collaboration with the client's product team, with your advisory role and a contract-economics-aware recommendation documented.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- All four named, non-growth portfolio items are present at the portfolio's "sufficient" bar minimum: the domain/strategy/market decision, the org-strategy-aligned team decision, the client feature evaluation with contract-economics reasoning, and the roadmap-participation/simplification pattern. Per `badges.md`, these four are the operative mandatory set for this badge; the account-growth-opportunity row is a separate, non-mandatory item carved out below, not one of the four required here.
- Within the four required items, the feature-evaluation item is non-substitutable *only if a feature-evaluation opportunity genuinely arose* during the period; if no client feature-evaluation opportunity arose at all through no fault of your assignment shape, this specific item is documented and routed through `badges.md` §1.7 rather than treated as a failed criterion — do not manufacture a feature evaluation that didn't really happen. This exception applies to the feature-evaluation item only, not to the other three required items.
- The account-growth-opportunity item (separate from the four required items above) is included if a genuine opportunity arose during the period; if none did, this is noted honestly per `badges.md` §1.7 rather than scored as a gap.
- A collaboration/facilitation observation has been arranged: a reviewer observes you in a real (or, if genuinely unavailable, simulated) roadmap/feature-evaluation discussion with the client's product team, checking for the collaborative behaviors named in the standard — deciding on work collaboratively, contributing when invited, evaluating features as an advisor, participating regularly, surfacing growth opportunities.
- Each portfolio item names the real client account, team(s), and counterparts involved — not anonymized to the point of being unverifiable.
- Where an artifact can't leave the client's environment, use the in-place attestation path (`badges.md` §1.5); where a client rotation cuts the roadmap-participation cycle short before a "regular" pattern is established, use `badges.md` §1.7.

**What a good solution looks like:** see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`'s "good" column for each of the five P5 rows — the throughline across all of them is that your specific judgment call, not just your presence, is identifiable in the artifact.

## Project 3 — Cross-team/cross-account strategic effort and growth plan, extending to an org-wide decision

**[P6 target, extending to P7 target]**

**Maps to sub-functions:** 1.5, 1.11, 1.12, 1.13, 1.14, 1.23, 1.24, 1.25, extends 1.11/1.24 and 1.13/1.14 for services context
**Builds toward badges:** SI-1-P6 (Cross-Account Strategic Multiplier) and SI-1-P7 (Organizational Strategy Steward)
**Rubric this is measured against:** `../assessment/pf1-business-acumen-strategy/03-portfolio-requirements.md` P6 and P7 rows, plus the P5/P6 collaboration observation and P7 teaching/communication demonstration described in "Review process"

**Description:** This project has two stages, run over real assigned strategic work — the P6 stage typically over 2–4 months, the P7 extension over a longer horizon and only when a genuinely org-scoped opportunity exists (it will not exist for every candidate in every window; see the acceptance criteria below rather than forcing one).

**Stage 1 [P6 target]:** Lead a real cross-team strategic effort to alignment on a major goal (potentially spanning more than one client account), and shape a real account-growth plan (expansion, cross-sell, or renewal) for one or more accounts, backed by either the §1.6 adoption-evidence artifact (named contact, dated reference, what concretely changed) or, if you're on a genuinely permanently-siloed single account, the §1.8 services-signal path as your *expected* route per §1.6a.

**Stage 2 [P7 target]:** Lead one specific organization-wide strategic decision or plan to alignment, and produce at least one of: (i) a cross-org roadmap co-owned with named product/business counterparts, (ii) a documented multi-account strategic-influence/growth record across ≥2 named accounts, or (iii) a one-off, high-stakes strategic decision with a documented major business consequence for LFT or the client — or an account-level trust/credibility claim attributed to a named client executive — backed by an independently-existing, dated artifact created at the time (not a retrospective account).

**Acceptance criteria (Stage 1 / P6):**
- The cross-team alignment effort is documented with the alignment actually reached, including how any initial disagreement was resolved — not just "we agreed."
- The account-growth plan names at least one real account and is either pursued to a documented outcome or, if pursued but not yet resolved, documented as in progress with next steps.
- If no growth/expansion/renewal opening arose in the account during the period through no fault of the candidate, this is documented and routed through `badges.md` §1.7, not treated as unearned.
- Where the same person would serve as both the §1.8 evidence source and the required Tier 5 co-signer (the common permanently-siloed case), the technical verifier independently interviews the cited client contact directly before sign-off — per `badges.md` §1.6a — and this interview is documented at sign-off.

**Acceptance criteria (Stage 2 / P7):**
- The org-wide decision/plan item is present and at "good" bar — alignment achieved and documented, including how disagreement was resolved. This item is non-substitutable; it is the one every P7 path shares.
- At least one of paths (i)/(ii)/(iii) is present at "good" bar. Path (iii), if used, requires the independently-existing dated artifact itself (not a description of it) submitted per `03-portfolio-requirements.md`'s submission format, and does not require an active crisis to qualify.
- A strategy teaching/communication demonstration has been arranged: presenting the decision, roadmap, or growth plan to a mixed technical/non-technical audience (including, where possible, a client-facing one), observed for whether the framing actually changed how the audience thought about the tradeoff — not just for clarity of delivery.
- If none of the three P7 sub-paths becomes available within one review cycle due to genuine assignment shape (not skill), this is routed through `badges.md` §1.7 rather than left open indefinitely — the manager/theme owner works a resolution (e.g. a temporary cross-account rotation).

**What a good solution looks like:** see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`'s P6 and P7 rows' "good" columns. The qualitative delta from Stage 1 to Stage 2 is not "the same kind of effort, bigger" — it's the shift from leading one cross-team effort to leading an effort where alignment has to hold across the whole organization, plus (for path iii specifically) evidence that a single decision carried real, documented business stakes.
