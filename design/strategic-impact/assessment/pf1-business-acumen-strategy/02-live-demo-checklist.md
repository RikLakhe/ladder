# PF 1 — Business Acumen & Strategy: Structured Conversation Checklist

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template calls this a "live demo checklist" for a hands-on technical scenario. This competency's Evidence Guide assesses P2–P4 through a structured conversation, not a live build — so this instrument is a guided interview occupying the same slot in the pipeline.

**Used when:** promotion review or interview (30–45 min structured conversation).
**Covers:** P2–P4. (P5–P7 are assessed via portfolio + a collaboration/facilitation or teaching observation — see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`.)

## Session: Business, strategy & product conversation

**Setup:** A single conversation covering the candidate's understanding of their team's business context, their awareness of and participation in engineering strategy, and their product judgment. 30–45 minutes.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Describes what their team's domain does and how it fits into the client account's business, in their own words, not a memorized description
- [ ] Can connect a specific piece of their work to a business or strategic goal, not just to a ticket or task
- [ ] Describes the organization's engineering strategy accurately (P3+), or the basic utility of the client's product (P2)
- [ ] **P3+:** can say whether their engagement is T&M or fixed-bid and give one concrete implication of that for how they scope or estimate work
- [ ] **P4 only:** describes a real decision where their understanding of the business model, contract economics, or adjacent-team domain changed the approach they took, a specific instance of roadmap feedback or a simplification opportunity they identified, and — if applicable — an account-growth or expansion opportunity they noticed and raised

**Prompts (level-differentiated):**
- P2: "What does the product you work on actually do for the people who use it?"
- P3: "How does your team's work connect to a goal you'd call 'strategic' rather than just 'on the sprint board'?" / "Is this engagement T&M or fixed-bid, and does that change how you approach scoping your own work?"
- P4: "Tell me about a decision where knowing more about the business or the contract's economics changed what you built or how." / "Describe a piece of roadmap feedback you gave, or a way you simplified a project once you understood the bigger picture." / "Have you ever noticed an opportunity for LFT to do more for this client — expand scope, bring in another capability? What did you do with that?"

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item; overall recommendation.
**Time budget:** 5 min intro, 25–35 min conversation, 5 min wrap-up.
