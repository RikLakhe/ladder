# PF 1 — Business Acumen & Strategy: Work Product Review Rubric

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template names this file `01-code-review-rubric.md` for a code-artifact review. This competency's work products are decisions, suggestions, and feedback notes rather than code, so this instrument is retitled — same slot in the pipeline, same pass-bar logic.

**Used when:** reviewing real artifacts a candidate has already produced (a documented decision, a written suggestion, meeting/feedback notes).
**Covers:** P2–P4. From P5 up, this PF is assessed via portfolio and a collaboration/facilitation observation (see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`) — strategic contribution and roadmap-shaping judgment show better in a fuller example than in a single artifact.

| Criteria | Does not meet P2 | Meets P2 | Meets P3 | Meets P4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business/domain understanding applied | Cannot describe how their team's work relates to the client account or what LFT delivers for it | Describes how their team's domain fits into the client account's business and the service LFT provides, when asked | Explains how their team's domain contributes to the client account's business goals and LFT's overall business strategy, unprompted or with light prompting | Applies domain understanding — including a basic grasp of an adjacent team's domain and the engagement's contract type (T&M vs. fixed-bid) — to a real, documented engineering decision |
| Strategic awareness & participation | _N/A at P2 — LFT matrix marks Strategic Work "n/a" at this level; do not penalize for it_ | _N/A at P2_ | Explains the organization's engineering strategy when asked, in their own words rather than reciting it | Has a documented contribution (meeting notes, written input, a decision record) to a discussion about what the org's engineering strategy implies for their team |
| Product judgment & account awareness | Cannot explain what the client's product they work on is for | Explains the basic utility of the client's product they work on | Has made at least one documented product-improvement suggestion (ticket, doc comment, or written note) | Has documented roadmap feedback given to the project team, a documented instance of identifying a project/technical simplification opportunity, and — where relevant — a documented instance of raising a potential account-growth or expansion opportunity |

**Pass bar:** all criteria at or above "Meets [target level]"; no criterion below "Meets" for the level being assessed. The Strategic Awareness row is excluded from the P2 pass bar entirely, not scored as a gap. The account-growth clause in the P4 Product Judgment row is scored on a good-faith basis — "where relevant" means don't penalize a candidate whose engagement genuinely offered no growth opening during the review period, but do probe for it before waiving it.

**For the candidate:**
- Have 1–2 real artifacts ready: a documented decision, a written product suggestion, or roadmap feedback notes you've actually given — not something written specially for this review
- If you have strategy-discussion notes (e.g. from a planning conversation where you contributed something), bring those too — they're what shows the "strategic awareness" row is a real contribution, not recitation
- Share your artifacts with your reviewer before the session so they can read beforehand; the 20–25 minutes is for discussion, not first-read
- A complete set for P3: at least one documented decision or suggestion, plus notes from a strategy discussion you contributed to. For P4: also include documented roadmap feedback, a documented simplification opportunity, and — if one came up — a note on an account-growth or expansion opportunity you raised
- If you don't yet have a real artifact for a criterion (e.g. no roadmap feedback given, or no growth opportunity arose during the period), say so — an honest gap is better than manufacturing one for the review

**How to run this review:**
- Time: 20–25 minutes
- Artifacts to collect: 1–2 documented decisions or suggestions, any strategy-discussion notes, and roadmap feedback records if available
- Who can assess: the candidate's manager, or any P5+ engineer who worked with them during the period being reviewed
