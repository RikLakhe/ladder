# PF 2 — Facilitation: Work Product Review Rubric

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template names this file `01-code-review-rubric.md` for a code-artifact review. This PF's work products are meeting/facilitation records, so this instrument is retitled — same slot in the pipeline, same pass-bar logic.

**Used when:** reviewing real meeting notes or facilitation records as a supplement to the live observation in `02-live-demo-checklist.md`.
**Covers:** P4–P5 (this PF is not applicable at P2–P3). This rubric is a secondary, corroborating instrument — the live observation is the primary assessment method for this PF at every level it applies to.
**Badges:** together with `02-live-demo-checklist.md`, this rubric is one evidence path for `LEAD-2-P4` and `LEAD-2-P5` — see `../badges.md`.

| Criteria | Does not meet P4 | Meets P4 (and P5 — same bar per the LFT matrix) |
|---|---|---|
| Balanced participation | Meeting notes/recordings show one or two voices dominating, with no visible attempt to draw others in | Notes or recollections show specific instances of drawing out quieter participants and redirecting a dominant one |
| Goal-tied outcomes | Meeting produced no clear outcome, or the outcome doesn't trace to a stated goal | Meeting notes show a decision or next step explicitly tied to a stated team goal |
| Client-facing facilitation (where applicable) | Notes from a client-attended session show only LFT participants managed for airtime/outcome, or no client-facing session is on record at all | At least one reviewed session includes client stakeholders, with notes showing the candidate drew out or managed both LFT and client participants |

**Pass bar:** all criteria at "Meets" — there is no separate P5 tier for these rows; the matrix holds P5 to the identical P4 bar. The client-facing row is scored "Meets" by default only if the candidate has no client-facing facilitation opportunity in their current role; where such an opportunity exists, at least one client-attended session must be reviewed. **Non-substitutable for `LEAD-2-P4` (added 2026-08-03):** the "Balanced participation" row is the criterion this badge's Certifies line most directly depends on ("a balanced/inclusive room"). It cannot be waived or averaged away against the other rows — a candidate cannot pass this badge with a HIGH gap on this row simply because the other rows are clean.

**How to run this review:**
- Time: 15 minutes for a single-session P4 review; ~30–40 minutes where a P5 range/reliability bundle (below) is being reviewed across sessions
- Artifacts to collect:
  - **P4:** notes from 1–2 meetings the candidate facilitated; where the candidate's role includes client-facing work, at least one of these should be a session with client stakeholders present.
  - **P5:** because the matrix holds P5 to the identical P4 bar and growth at this level shows as range and reliability rather than a new behavior (see `../standard/2-facilitation.md`), collect notes from **≥3 real facilitated sessions across one review cycle**, with at least one team-only session and (where the role permits) one client-facing session, each independently scored against the same P4 criteria above. This is the evidence bundle `LEAD-2-P5` in `../badges.md` is awarded against — do not substitute a single session or a 1–2 session sample at P5. **Where facilitation is not the candidate's primary billable role and the assignment structurally cannot generate 3 qualifying sessions in one review cycle, mark `LEAD-2-P5` "blocked — assignment-limited" per §1.7 (see `../badges.md`) rather than unearned** — collect and preserve whatever partial session record exists toward the next cycle.
- Who can assess: the candidate's manager or a P5+ engineer who attended one of the reviewed meetings
