# PF 2 — Facilitation: Portfolio Requirements

**Used when:** promotion review to P7, or architect-level hiring. (P6 is primarily assessed via the live cross-team facilitation scenario in `02-live-demo-checklist.md`; this portfolio requirement supplements it with a real, sustained example.)
**Badges:** the P7 rows below are the evidence base for `LEAD-2-P7`; the P6 row supplements `LEAD-2-P6`'s live-demo evidence — see `../badges.md`.

## Required evidence items

| Item | Maps to PC | Quality bar: sufficient | Quality bar: good |
|---|---|---|---|
| A cross-team discussion facilitated to a decision with buy-in | 2.2, 2.3 [P6] | Meeting documented with a decision and named participants | Both sides confirm the decision stuck and was genuinely agreed to, not just imposed |
| A client-facing session facilitated to a goal-tied outcome | 2.1 or 2.2/2.3 depending on level [P4+] | One documented ceremony or working session with client stakeholders present, with a decision or next step named | A client stakeholder or the account lead confirms the session was well-run and the outcome held |
| (P7) An organization-wide discussion facilitated | 2.4 [P7] | Discussion documented, org-wide participation confirmed | Discussion is cited elsewhere as a well-run example others reference or model their own facilitation on |
| (P7) A discussion facilitated across more than one client account | 2.4 [P7] | Discussion documented spanning two or more client engagements, with a decision or shared standard named | The outcome is adopted across the accounts involved, not just agreed to in the room |

## Submission format

- Link to meeting notes, recording, or a written summary with named participants
- 1-paragraph context: what was at stake, why facilitation (rather than just a decision handed down) was the right approach
- Where client stakeholders were present: name the authority dynamic in the room (e.g., a client decision-maker outranking LFT attendees) and how it was navigated
- For P7: name the parts of the organization or the client accounts involved and any friction encountered

## Review process

- Reviewed by a P6+ engineer not on the candidate's team, plus the candidate's manager
- 30-minute review session
- Questions to ask: "What would you do differently if you ran this again?" "How did you know you had real buy-in and not just silence?"
- **P7 only:** add a teaching/facilitation demonstration — candidate teaches a facilitation technique to a group; reviewer observes for clarity and evidence of adoption
- For `LEAD-2-P7`'s multi-account claim, the §1.6 adoption artifact (or the §1.8 services-signal alternative) named in `../badges.md` is required, not just a description of the discussion
