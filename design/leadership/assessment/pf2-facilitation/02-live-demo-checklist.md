# PF 2 — Facilitation: Live Demo Checklist

**Used when:** technical/promotion interview or observed real meeting (40–60 min session). This is the primary assessment method for this PF — facilitation is a skill best watched happening, not discussed retrospectively.
**Covers:** P4, P5 (identical bar per the LFT matrix's "P5: See P4"), P6. (P7 is assessed via portfolio + a teaching/facilitation demonstration — see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`.)
**Badges:** this checklist is the primary evidence path for `LEAD-2-P4`, `LEAD-2-P5`, and `LEAD-2-P6` — see `../badges.md`.

## Scenario: P4/P5 — Team discussion facilitation

**Setup:** Candidate facilitates a discussion with 3–4 participants (real teammates or interviewers role-playing) on a topic with some disagreement built in — e.g., "how should we prioritize these three competing bugs?" One participant is briefed to be quiet unless drawn out; another is briefed to dominate unless managed. 30–40 minutes.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Actively draws out the quiet participant at least once **— mandatory/non-substitutable for `LEAD-2-P4` (see `../badges.md`): this and the next item are the checklist items most directly tied to the badge's "balanced/inclusive room" claim and cannot be traded off against the other items**
- [ ] Manages the dominant participant without shutting them down harshly **— mandatory/non-substitutable for `LEAD-2-P4`, same basis as above**
- [ ] Ties the discussion's outcome back to a stated goal or decision criterion
- [ ] Keeps the discussion on topic, using a time-box or redirect when it drifts

**Prompts:**
- "How did you decide when to step in versus let the discussion run?"

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item. For `LEAD-2-P4`, items 1 and 2 above must both pass — a 3-of-4 pass that includes a fail on either of these two does not clear the badge, even though it would clear a generic ≥3/4 threshold. **Time budget:** 5 min setup, 30 min session, 5 min debrief.

## Scenario: P4/P5 — Client-facing facilitation (use where the candidate's role is client-facing)

**Setup:** Candidate facilitates a working session or ceremony with 2–3 participants role-playing client stakeholders, including one briefed to assert authority they don't formally have over the candidate's process (e.g., insisting on a shortcut, pulling rank on scope). One LFT teammate participant is also present. 30–40 minutes.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Manages both LFT and client participants' airtime, not just the LFT side of the room **— mandatory/non-substitutable for `LEAD-2-P4` where this scenario is part of the evidence bundle: this is the client-facing equivalent of the balanced/inclusive-room claim**
- [ ] Holds the discussion to a goal-tied outcome even when a client participant pushes for an unstated priority
- [ ] Responds to the client participant's assertion of authority without either capitulating on quality or becoming adversarial
- [ ] Closes with a decision or next step both sides can point to, not an outcome the client attendee simply dictated

**Prompts:**
- "How did you handle it when [the client stakeholder] pushed back — what made you hold the line or adapt?"

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item. **Time budget:** 5 min setup, 30 min session, 5 min debrief. Where a candidate's role has no client-facing component, this scenario is skipped and noted as not applicable rather than scored as a gap. For `LEAD-2-P4`, per `../badges.md`, this session requires the delivery/account manager as co-signer when included in the evidence bundle, and item 1 above is mandatory/non-substitutable within this scenario's own scoring.

## Scenario: P6 — Cross-team discussion facilitation

**Setup:** Candidate facilitates a discussion between representatives of two "teams" (interviewers) with a genuine disagreement about a shared dependency or timeline. 45–50 minutes.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Ensures both teams' relevant perspectives are represented in the room before starting
- [ ] Guides the discussion toward an actual decision, not just an exchange of positions
- [ ] Explicitly checks for and secures buy-in from both sides before closing (not just assumed agreement)

**Prompts:**
- "If one side had quietly disagreed with the outcome, how would you have known?"

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item. **Time budget:** 5 min setup, 35 min session, 5 min debrief.

## Scenario: P6 — Cross-account facilitation with a client decision-maker (use where the candidate's role spans client engagements)

**Setup:** Candidate facilitates a discussion involving LFT participants and a role-played client decision-maker who outranks everyone else in the room, where LFT and client priorities genuinely conflict (e.g., a scope or timeline disagreement). 45–50 minutes.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Surfaces the actual disagreement rather than letting the client decision-maker's rank pre-empt discussion
- [ ] Guides toward a decision that reflects genuine input from both sides, not one the client attendee simply hands down
- [ ] Explicitly checks for buy-in from LFT participants as well as the client decision-maker before closing

**Prompts:**
- "How did you tell the difference between the client agreeing and the client just outranking the room?"

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item. **Time budget:** 5 min setup, 35 min session, 5 min debrief. Not applicable where the candidate's role has no client-account-spanning component. Where used as evidence for `LEAD-2-P6`, this scenario's buy-in claim still requires the §1.6-style corroboration named in `../badges.md`, not just a passed checklist.
