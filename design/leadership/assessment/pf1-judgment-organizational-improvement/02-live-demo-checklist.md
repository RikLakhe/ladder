# PF 1 — Judgment & Organizational Improvement: Structured Conversation Checklist

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template calls this a "live demo checklist" for a hands-on technical scenario. This PF's Evidence Guide assesses P2–P4 through a structured conversation, not a live build — so this instrument is a guided interview occupying the same slot in the pipeline.

**Used when:** promotion review or interview (30–40 min structured conversation).
**Covers:** P2–P4. (P5–P7 are assessed via portfolio + a manager/peer observation — see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`.)
**Badges:** this checklist, together with `01-work-product-review-rubric.md`, is the evidence base for `LEAD-1-P2` through `LEAD-1-P4` — see `../badges.md`.

## Session: Judgment & organizational-improvement conversation

**Setup:** A conversation covering a real decision, a bias they've noticed in themselves, a strategy-linked conversation they've had, and a process improvement they've discussed. 30–40 minutes.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Can name a specific decision-making bias and explain why it matters, even in the abstract (P2 baseline)
- [ ] Describes a real decision where they noticed and corrected for a bias in their own thinking
- [ ] Can explain what accountability for decisions and outcomes means, even in the abstract (P2 baseline)
- [ ] Describes holding themselves accountable for a real outcome, including one that didn't go as planned
- [ ] Describes a real conversation connecting their work to organizational strategy or goals (P2 baseline: contributed to one, when appropriate). **If no such conversation occurred at all during the review window, mark this item N/A per §1.7 (see `../badges.md`) rather than fail** — this is a single-occurrence dependency the candidate may simply not have had the opportunity for, not a skill gap.
- [ ] Can explain their team's current practices and processes, when asked (P2 baseline)
- [ ] Describes at least one process improvement they've discussed with the team, even if only once (P3)
- [ ] **P4 only:** describes a conversation held specifically to ensure team alignment, and a process improvement discussed regularly (not a one-off) with the team
- [ ] **P4 only:** describes a concrete way they check whether their team's work is still tracking towards its shared goals (1.17) — not just a general sense that things are fine
- [ ] **P4 only:** where the team is delivering to a client, can name at least one practice or process the client's contract, tooling, or governance constrains, and distinguish it from what the team can change on its own

**Prompts (level-differentiated):**
- P2: "What's a bias that can affect how engineers make technical decisions? Why does it matter?"
- P2 (accountability): "What does it mean to be accountable for a decision and its outcome, even one that didn't go well?"
- P2 (process): "Walk me through your team's current practices and processes — what are they, and why do you do them that way?"
- P3: "Tell me about a decision where you caught yourself being biased, and what you did about it."
- P3 (process): "Tell me about a process improvement you raised with the team, even if it was just once."
- P4: "Tell me about a conversation where you connected your team's work to the bigger strategic picture — what made you bring it up?"
- P4 (client constraint): "What's a practice or tool on this engagement that's dictated by the client rather than something your team chose? How do you tell the difference?"

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item; overall recommendation. The strategy-conversation item scores N/A (not fail) where no such conversation arose in the window at all — see the item note above.
**Time budget:** 5 min intro, 25–30 min conversation, 5 min wrap-up.
