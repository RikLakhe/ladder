# PF 1 — Judgment & Organizational Improvement: Work Product Review Rubric

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template names this file `01-code-review-rubric.md` for a code-artifact review. This PF's work products are decision records and process proposals rather than code, so this instrument is retitled — same slot in the pipeline, same pass-bar logic.

**Used when:** reviewing real, documented decisions and process-improvement discussions.
**Covers:** P2–P4. From P5 up, this PF is assessed via portfolio and a manager/peer observation (see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`).
**Badges:** clearing this rubric's rows at a given level is the evidence base for `LEAD-1-P2` through `LEAD-1-P4` — see `../badges.md`.

| Criteria | Does not meet P2 | Meets P2 | Meets P3 | Meets P4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Decision-making & accountability | Cannot explain what bias or accountability mean in the context of a decision | Explains how bias affects decision-making and what accountability means, when asked | Has a documented decision where they reflected on their own bias and held themselves accountable for the outcome | Same bar as P3 — this is a day-one expectation carried through P4 per the LFT matrix's combined P3–P4 row; check for continued evidence, not a new tier |
| Strategy & goal alignment | Cannot connect team work to any stated organizational goal | Has contributed to a conversation referencing organizational strategy or principles, when appropriate. **If no strategy-referencing conversation occurred at all during the review window, mark this row N/A per §1.7 (see `../badges.md`) rather than "does not meet"** — this is a single-occurrence dependency, not a skill gap. | Has initiated such a conversation themselves, unprompted | Has a documented conversation that connected organizational strategy to team alignment specifically |
| Goal-tracking | N/A — not assessed before P4 | N/A — not assessed before P4 | N/A — not assessed before P4 | Has a concrete, documented way of checking whether their team's work is continuously tracking towards its shared goals (1.17) — not just a general sense that things are fine |
| Process improvement | Has never suggested or discussed a process improvement | Can explain their team's practices and processes when asked | Has discussed at least one process improvement with the team | Regularly discusses process improvements with the team (more than a single instance) |
| Client-constrained improvement (P4 only, informal) | N/A — not a matrix expectation before P4 | N/A | N/A | Where the team's practices are shaped by a client's tooling or process mandate, can explain what is and isn't within the team's control to change — this is a day-one framing check, not yet the full P5 skill assessed via portfolio |

**Pass bar:** all criteria at or above "Meets [target level]"; no criterion below "Meets" for the level being assessed. The Decision-Making row does not gain a new tier between P3 and P4 — don't penalize a candidate for "not growing" there; check instead that the habit has continued. The Strategy & goal alignment row's P2 cell is scored N/A, not "does not meet," when no qualifying conversation occurred in the window at all — see the cell note.

**How to run this review:**
- Time: 20–25 minutes
- Artifacts to collect: 1–2 decision records or documented decisions, any notes from strategy-linked planning conversations, any process-improvement discussion notes
- Who can assess: the candidate's manager or any P5+ engineer familiar with their decision-making
