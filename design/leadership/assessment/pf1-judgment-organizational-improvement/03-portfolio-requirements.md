# PF 1 — Judgment & Organizational Improvement: Portfolio Requirements

**Used when:** promotion review to P5/P6/P7, or lead/principal/architect-level hiring.
**Badges:** clearing this portfolio's P5, P6, and P7 rows is the evidence base for `LEAD-1-P5`, `LEAD-1-P6`, `LEAD-1-P7`, and the crisis-distinct `LEAD-1-P7-CRISIS` — see `../badges.md` for exact evidence bundles, completion bars, and the confidentiality/assignment-limited/services-signal paths (§1.5–§1.8) that apply when a portfolio item depends on a specific client engagement.

## Submission format

- Link to decision records, strategy-conversation notes, or process-improvement documentation
- 1-paragraph context: the situation, what changed, and how the outcome is known (not just asserted)
- For P5+ items involving a client engagement: name what was within the candidate's control to change directly, what required client negotiation, and what had to be worked around
- For P6/P7: name the teams or client accounts involved and any resistance encountered

## Review process

- Reviewed by a P6+ engineer not on the candidate's team, plus the candidate's manager
- 30-minute review session
- Questions to ask: "What decision have you gotten wrong, and how did you find out?" "What resistance did you get to this process change, and how did you handle it?" "Tell me about a time a client's existing tooling or process blocked the change you wanted — what did you do instead?"
- **P7 only:** add a teaching/culture demonstration — candidate presents a decision-making, alignment, or process framework to a group outside their usual circle
- **Where a candidate raises a genuine account-crisis story:** treat it as a separate item against `LEAD-1-P7-CRISIS`, not folded into the standard P7 rows — it requires the delivery/account manager's independent confirmation and an independent pre-crisis artifact per that badge's row in `../badges.md`
