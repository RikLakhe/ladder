# PF 3 — Mentoring: Portfolio Requirements

**Used when:** promotion review to P5/P6/P7, or lead/principal/architect-level hiring.
**Badges:** clearing the rows below is the evidence base for `LEAD-3-P5`, `LEAD-3-P6`, and `LEAD-3-P7` — see `../badges.md`, including the §1.7 "blocked — assignment-limited" path that applies at every level here when no mentee/coachee is currently available, and the §1.6a permanently-siloed-account path for P6/P7.

## Required evidence items

| Item | Maps to PC | Quality bar: sufficient | Quality bar: good |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence of mentoring beyond one's own team, as needed | 3.4 [P5] | One documented mentoring relationship with someone outside the team | Mentee (or their manager) confirms real developmental value, not just occasional contact |
| Evidence of sustaining mentoring under utilization pressure | 3.4 [P5] | One documented mentoring relationship that continued through at least one period of high chargeable-work demand | The candidate can describe a concrete step they took to protect the time (e.g., negotiating with a Staff Manager, fixed weekly slot) rather than the relationship surviving by luck |
| Evidence of mentoring across teams as a matter of course | 3.5 [P6] | Two or more concurrent or sequential cross-team mentoring relationships | A recognizable pattern — the candidate is sought out by people outside their team specifically for mentoring |
| Evidence of fostering a cross-team mentoring culture | 3.8 [P6] | One documented instance of connecting someone else to a mentoring opportunity | A recurring practice — the candidate actively matches mentors and mentees, not just mentors personally |
| (P7) Evidence of mentoring across the organization | 3.6 [P7] | One org-wide mentoring relationship or program participation | Sought out organization-wide as a mentor, with multiple relationships documented |
| (P7) Evidence of fostering an organizational mentoring culture | 3.9 [P7] | One org-level mentoring initiative (a program, a matching system) | The initiative is sustained and adopted beyond the candidate's direct involvement |
| (P7) Evidence of supporting others in their growth as mentors | 3.10 [P7] | One documented instance of coaching someone else's mentoring practice | A mentor the candidate coached is now independently effective, with a before/after account |

## Submission format

- Link to any documentation of the mentoring relationship, program, or coaching instance
- 1-paragraph context: who was involved, what changed, and how it's known (a mentee/peer account, not just the candidate's own description)
- Where the mentee was rotating onto a new client engagement: name the codebase/stack transition and how long it took to reach productive contribution
- For P6/P7: name the teams/organization-wide/client-account scope involved

## Review process

- Reviewed by a P6+ engineer not on the candidate's team, plus the candidate's manager
- 30-minute review session; where possible, a brief input from a mentee is gathered beforehand
- Questions to ask: "What's a mentoring relationship that didn't work well, and what did you learn?" "How do you know the person you mentored actually grew, versus just enjoyed the conversations?" "Tell me about a mentoring relationship that came under pressure when billable work picked up — what happened to it?"
- **P7 only:** add a teaching/coaching-the-coach demonstration — candidate coaches another mentor's practice in an observed session, or presents a mentoring framework to a group
- **Where a candidate has had no one to coach as a mentor this cycle:** do not score the coaching-the-coach item as a gap — apply §1.7 (assignment-limited) per `LEAD-3-P7` in `../badges.md`
