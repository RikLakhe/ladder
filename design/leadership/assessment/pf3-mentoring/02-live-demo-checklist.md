# PF 3 — Mentoring: Structured Conversation Checklist

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template calls this a "live demo checklist" for a hands-on technical scenario. This PF's Evidence Guide assesses P2–P4 through a structured conversation, not a live build — so this instrument is a guided interview occupying the same slot in the pipeline.

**Used when:** promotion review or interview (25–35 min structured conversation).
**Covers:** P2–P4. (P5–P7 are assessed via portfolio + a mentee/peer reference — see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`.)
**Badges:** this checklist, together with `01-work-product-review-rubric.md`, is the evidence base for `LEAD-3-P2` through `LEAD-3-P4` — see `../badges.md`.

## Session: Mentoring conversation

**Setup:** A conversation covering mentorship the candidate has sought and, once applicable, provided. 25–35 minutes.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Describes a specific mentor they've sought out and a specific thing they gained from it (P2 baseline)
- [ ] Describes a specific instance of mentoring a teammate, not a general "I try to help people" statement
- [ ] Can describe what "open, respectful, flexible, and empathetic" mentoring looks like in practice, with a real example
- [ ] **P3/P4 delta:** can state how often, and over what period, they've mentored a teammate — a single or occasional instance is sufficient for P3, but P4 requires describing an ongoing, reliable pattern rather than one occasion
- [ ] **P4 only:** describes a mentoring relationship built specifically to reduce a bus-factor risk or build backfill capability, and what capability actually transferred
- [ ] **P4 only (where applicable):** describes mentoring someone onto an unfamiliar client codebase or stack, and how they compressed the ramp-up to productive contribution

**If the candidate has had no teammate to mentor this review cycle** (e.g., placed solo on a client team with no junior engineers), do not score the mentoring-instance items as failed — note "no mentee available" and route the badge decision through §1.7 (assignment-limited) in `../badges.md` rather than treating it as a skill gap.

**Prompts (level-differentiated):**
- P2: "Tell me about a mentor you sought out. What did you specifically get from that relationship?"
- P3: "Tell me about a time you mentored a teammate, even informally."
- P3/P4 (frequency): "How often has this happened, and over what period? Was it a one-off, occasional, or an ongoing pattern?"
- P4: "Tell me about a mentoring relationship where the goal was building redundancy on your team. What happened?"
- P4 (client ramp-up): "Tell me about helping someone get up to speed on a client codebase or stack they hadn't worked in before. What did you do to speed that up?"

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item; overall recommendation.
**Time budget:** 5 min intro, 20–25 min conversation, 5 min wrap-up.
