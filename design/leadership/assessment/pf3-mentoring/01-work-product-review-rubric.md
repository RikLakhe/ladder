# PF 3 — Mentoring: Work Product Review Rubric

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template names this file `01-code-review-rubric.md` for a code-artifact review. This PF's work products are mentoring relationship records, so this instrument is retitled — same slot in the pipeline, same pass-bar logic.

**Used when:** reviewing real, documented mentoring relationships.
**Covers:** P2–P4. From P5 up, this PF is assessed via portfolio and a mentee/peer reference (see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`).
**Badges:** this rubric is the evidence base for `LEAD-3-P2` through `LEAD-3-P4` — see `../badges.md`, including the §1.7 "blocked — assignment-limited" path that applies at P3/P4 when no mentee is currently available.

| Criteria | Does not meet P2 | Meets P2 | Meets P3 | Meets P4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mentoring relationship | No evidence of seeking or providing mentorship | Describes a mentor they've sought out and what they gained from the relationship | Has a documented instance of mentoring a teammate, even informally and occasionally | Has a documented, ongoing mentoring relationship with a teammate, conducted in an open, respectful, flexible, and empathetic manner |
| Redundancy/backfill purpose | _N/A at P2–P3 — this purpose is not a matrix expectation until P4; do not penalize for its absence_ | _N/A_ | _N/A_ | Has a documented instance of mentoring specifically aimed at building team redundancy or backfill capability, with a concrete capability transfer identifiable |
| Client-context ramp-up (P4 only, where applicable) | _N/A before P4_ | _N/A_ | _N/A_ | Where the candidate's team rotates engineers onto new client engagements, has a documented instance of mentoring someone onto an unfamiliar client codebase or stack, with time-to-productive-contribution identifiable |

**Pass bar:** all criteria at or above "Meets [target level]"; no criterion below "Meets" for the level being assessed. The redundancy/backfill row is excluded from the P2/P3 pass bar entirely, not scored as a gap. Where the "Mentoring relationship" row is unmet at P3/P4 solely because the candidate has had no teammate available to mentor this cycle, mark the row "blocked — assignment-limited" per §1.7 in `../badges.md` rather than "does not meet."

**How to run this review:**
- Time: 15–20 minutes
- Artifacts to collect: any documented mentoring relationship (1:1 notes, growth plan), and the mentee's perspective if available
- Who can assess: the candidate's manager, or the mentee/mentor themselves via a short input
