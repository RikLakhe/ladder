- [ ] Has read `../../my-role.md` and `../../goals/company-okr-2026.md` — the concrete artifacts this competency's "organizational strategy" and "goals" language traces to
- [ ] Has skimmed `../assessment/badges.md` Part 1 (the generic badge mechanics — §1.5 confidentiality, §1.5a access-loss, §1.6 adoption evidence, §1.6a permanently-siloed roles, §1.7 assignment-limited, §1.8 services-signal) — every exercise and project below references one or more of these, and they're easier to recognize having seen once up front than discovered ad hoc at review time

## Level gates

- **P2 → P3:** passes the work-product review rubrics at "Meets P3" for PF 1 and PF 3, and has held the bias-check and mentorship-seeking habits (Exercises 1 and 5) for at least a couple of weeks. Badge equivalent: `LEAD-1-P3`, `LEAD-3-P3`.
- **P3 → P4:** passes "Meets P4" on PF 1's and PF 3's rubrics — the full 4-piece bundle for `LEAD-1-P4` (strategy conversation, goal-tracking artifact, ≥2 process-improvement notes, client-constraint framing note), not any one piece alone — and passes the PF 2 live facilitation scenario (this PF first applies at P4). Badge equivalent: `LEAD-1-P4`, `LEAD-2-P4`, `LEAD-3-P4`.
- **P4 → P5:** scope-of-role transition (execution → ownership/culture) — not producible by a guided exercise alone. Gate is the Phase D portfolio requirements for P5 across PF 1 and PF 3 (PF 2 has no new P5 bar — the matrix holds it to the P4 standard, but `LEAD-2-P5` still requires the ≥3-session range bundle from Exercise 13). Portfolio evidence must include the mandatory client-constraint item for `LEAD-1-P5` (Exercise 9 — non-substitutable, per `05-badge-reference-card.md`) and the sustained-under-pressure item for `LEAD-3-P5` (Exercise 12). Autonomous Projects 1 and 3 are reasonable on-ramps, not substitutes for the portfolio review.
- **P5 → P6 → P7:** same pattern, scaling scope from team to several teams to organization. See each standard's Promotion Criteria section — there is no content beyond what's listed here; from P6 the primary readiness signal shifts to portfolio review, supplemented by the P6-stretch exercise and projects already in this sequence (Exercise 4, and Autonomous Projects 1–3). At P7, `LEAD-3-P7` additionally requires the coaching-the-coach item (Exercise 14) — do not treat a strong org-wide mentoring record alone as sufficient for that badge. `LEAD-1-P7-CRISIS` is a separate, situational achievement layered on top of (not required for) `LEAD-1-P7` — see Autonomous Project 4. Readiness is demonstrated through actual cross-team/org-wide leadership, assessed via portfolio + (at P7) a teaching/culture demonstration.

## Skip guide

- **Experienced hire joining at P4 or above:** skip concept notes and guided exercises entirely. Start at the relevant Autonomous Project, and use the Phase D portfolio requirements (and `05-badge-reference-card.md`) to identify what evidence to bring from their prior role.
- **Experienced hire joining at P3:** skim concept notes as a refresher, go straight to Exercises 2 and 6 to confirm baseline, then proceed to the autonomous projects.
- **Anyone who has held a team-lead or EM-adjacent role before:** skip Exercise 3 (facilitation is likely already a strong habit), but still do Exercises 1, 2, and 6 — LFT-specific bias/accountability norms, process-improvement practice, and mentoring purpose (redundancy/backfill) won't transfer automatically from a prior company. Do not skip Exercise 13 even if facilitation itself is strong — `LEAD-2-P5` is about the *bundle*, not the skill, and a prior-company facilitation habit doesn't come with an LFT-dated ≥3-session log already attached.
- **Anyone targeting P5 or P7 who assumes their strongest portfolio items alone are enough:** do not skip Exercise 9 (P5) or Exercise 14 (P7) — both build a specific badge item the redesign made explicitly mandatory and non-substitutable (see `05-badge-reference-card.md`'s self-check note).
