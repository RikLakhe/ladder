# Leadership — Badge Reference Card

**Purpose:** a one-screen lookup so a candidate or buddy can see, at a glance, which training unit prepares which of the 17 Leadership badges — without re-reading all nine assessment instruments across the three PFs. This card does not redefine anything; every cell links back to `../assessment/badges.md` Part 2 and the instrument it names. If this card and an assessment instrument ever disagree, the instrument in `../assessment/` wins.

**Reuse note (for future competencies):** this file's *shape* — one row per badge, linking training unit → evidence artifact → instrument — is the reusable pattern, copied from Technical Skill's, Delivery's, and Feedback/Communication & Collaboration's `05-badge-reference-card.md`. Only the PF names, badge IDs, and artifact names are specific to this competency.

---

## How to read this card

- **Badge ID** and **Name** — from `../assessment/badges.md` Part 2.
- **Training unit that builds it** — which guided exercise, autonomous project, or onboarding milestone in this `training/` folder produces (or extends) the required evidence artifact.
- **Evidence artifact required** — restated from the badge's "Evidence required" field, so you know what to walk away from the training unit holding.
- **Instrument** — which file in `../assessment/` actually awards the badge.

Training builds the artifact. It does not award the badge — only a Verifier at the role/level named in the instrument can do that (see `../assessment/badges.md` §1.3), including the required co-signer at Tier 3 (P4) and Tier 5 (P6), and the always-required co-signer on `LEAD-1-P7-CRISIS`.

## Self-check performed on this card (2026-08-03 redesign)

- **All 17 badges have a named training unit** whose output artifact matches the badge's "Evidence required" field — including the two that had no training unit before this pass: `LEAD-2-P5`'s ≥3-session bundle (now Guided Exercise 13) and `LEAD-3-P7`'s coaching-the-coach item (now Guided Exercise 14).
- **`LEAD-1-P7-CRISIS` is deliberately marked as not exercise-producible** — see Autonomous Project 4's note. It is the one badge in the catalog whose training unit is preparation/documentation guidance rather than a rehearsable artifact, because the badge's own award rule forbids a constructed exercise from substituting for a genuine crisis.
- **Sequencing checked:** no guided exercise requires an artifact built only in a later exercise. Exercise 13 (P5 bundle) explicitly depends on Exercise 3/10 (single-session skill) preceding it, not the reverse. Exercise 14 (coaching-the-coach) depends on the coached mentor already having a real mentoring relationship in progress (built via Exercise 5/6 by that mentor, not by the candidate) — the candidate coaches, they don't first build the mentee relationship themselves.
- **`LEAD-1-P5`'s mandatory item 5 is flagged non-substitutable** everywhere it's referenced in this training package (Exercise 9, Project 1, this card) — a candidate cannot clear `LEAD-1-P5` on the strength of the other four portfolio items alone.

## What this card is not

- Not a substitute for reading the actual rubric/checklist/portfolio file before an assessment — it tells you where to look, not what the bar is in full.
- Not a self-certification tool — see `../assessment/badges.md` §1.3: a badge requires a named Verifier's sign-off, always, plus the co-signer at Tier 3/Tier 5 (and always, for `LEAD-1-P7-CRISIS`) where noted above.
