# Leadership — Onboarding Track

For a new hire (or an existing engineer newly picking up this competency) who needs to get up to speed fast. See `05-badge-reference-card.md` for which badge each milestone below is building toward.

## Day 1

**Read:**
- `../../lft-engineering-competency-matrix.md` Section 4 "Leadership" — the source of truth this competency is built from
- Concept notes §1 and §10 (`01-concept-notes.md`) — cognitive bias literacy and the mentoring-vs-managing distinction
- `../../COMPANY-CONTEXT.md` — the team and organizational context this competency operates in

**Do:** confirm who their onboarding buddy is (a natural first mentoring relationship, from the mentee side — builds toward `LEAD-3-P2`). Note: this onboarding is almost always onto a specific client account, not a generic internal team — expect concept notes §15–18 (client-constrained improvement, facilitating across the LFT-client boundary, fast-ramp mentoring, mentoring under utilization pressure) to become relevant sooner than the "as it comes up" pacing below implies, if the new hire is ramping onto an active client engagement.

## Week 1

**Do:**
- Start Guided Exercise 1 (bias-check decision journal) — begin the habit immediately (builds toward `LEAD-1-P2`/`LEAD-1-P3`)
- Start Guided Exercise 5 (mentorship-seeking) — have a first real conversation with their buddy or another mentor about a genuine challenge (builds toward `LEAD-3-P2`)

**Read:** concept notes §3 (`../../my-role.md` and `../../goals/company-okr-2026.md`) so organizational strategy language isn't unfamiliar later.

## Month 1

**Deliver:**
- Complete Guided Exercise 1's two-week journal and review it with their manager
- Guided Exercise 2 (process-improvement proposal practice) on a real piece of team friction they've noticed (builds toward `LEAD-1-P3`)
- Look for a first opportunity to informally mentor someone even more junior, if one exists (Exercise 5's second half, toward `LEAD-3-P3`)

**Read:** remaining concept notes (§2, §4–9, §11–15, §17–18) as the relevant exercises, meetings, or mentoring situations come up, rather than all at once. Facilitation-specific notes (§6–9, §16) become relevant once they're facilitating their own meetings — for a new hire ramping onto a client account, that can include client-facing ceremonies early on, so don't assume this is a later-month topic by default. If their onboarding buddy relationship is itself an instance of fast-ramp mentoring onto a client codebase (concept note §17), that's expected and worth naming as such.

## Buddy check — 2-week check-in questions

- "Tell me about a decision you made recently — what bias might have been in play, and did you check for it?"
- "Have you had a real conversation with a mentor yet? What did you talk about?"
- "Is there a team practice or process that's already bugging you? What would you change about it?"
- "Is there anything about how decisions get made or how the team improves its own practices that still feels unclear or arbitrary?" (This question is for catching onboarding gaps, not testing the new hire — treat the answer as feedback on onboarding quality, not a mark against them.)
- "Is anything about how decisions or process changes work here different because it's the client's account, not a purely internal team? What's confusing about that boundary so far?"

## Note for new hires joining at P5+ or being fast-tracked

- This onboarding track is written for someone starting at P2–P4. A P5+ hire should still do Day 1/Week 1 (context-setting is universal), but should move straight to `00-learning-path.md`'s "Skip guide" and the relevant Autonomous Project rather than working through Month 1's exercises — and should be made aware early that `LEAD-1-P5`'s client-constraint item (Guided Exercise 9) and, if targeting P7 eventually, `LEAD-3-P7`'s coaching-the-coach item (Guided Exercise 14) are mandatory, non-substitutable pieces of their eventual badge evidence, not optional extras to pick up "if time allows."
