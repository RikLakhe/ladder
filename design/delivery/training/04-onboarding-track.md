# Delivery — Onboarding Track

For a new hire (or an existing engineer newly picking up this competency) who needs to get up to speed fast.

**Badge context:** by the end of Month 1, a P2 new hire should have accumulated the evidence bundle for DL-1-P2 (a ≥5-ticket delivery-unit bundle) and be partway or fully through the continuous cycle DL-2-P2 requires (a full 2–4 week status/escalation record). Neither of these can be assembled retroactively at review time — see `05-badge-reference-card.md`. None of this is awarded automatically by finishing the track — a Verifier still reviews the real artifacts produced along the way, per `../assessment/badges.md` §1.3.

## Day 1

**Read:**
- `../../lft-engineering-competency-matrix.md` Section 2 "Delivery" — the source of truth this competency is built from
- Concept notes §1–3 (`01-concept-notes.md`) — incremental delivery principles, sizing heuristics, dependency mapping
- `../../COMPANY-CONTEXT.md` — the team's backlog/ticket tooling and delivery norms
- `05-badge-reference-card.md` — skim once so the new hire sees what "done" eventually looks like (a real bundle, a real cycle record), without needing to read every assessment instrument on Day 1

**Do:** get access to the team's backlog tool and standup/status channel, and confirm you know how to pull a dated history from both (not just view the current state) — the redesigned rubrics check timestamps, not recollection. Confirm who their onboarding buddy is; find out which client engagement they're joining, its contract type (fixed-bid vs T&M), and whether ceremonies/tooling/reporting are set by the client or by LFT — this shapes how status communication and scope decisions work here, and whether the eventual bundle/cycle record needs the in-place attestation path (`badges.md` §1.5) instead of an exported copy.

**Set the bundle/cycle clock now, not later:** identify, on Day 1, what delivery unit (sprint/epic slice/ramp period) will become the Exercise 1/2 bundle, and what 2–4 week span will become the Exercise 3/4 cycle. Both need a defined start point — don't let this drift into "whichever tickets look good in retrospect."

## Week 1

**Do:**
- Start Guided Exercise 1 (delivery-unit bundle: task sizing & splitting) on the first real tickets of the delivery unit picked on Day 1, with their buddy reviewing each proposed split as it happens
- Start Guided Exercise 3 (2–4 week status/escalation cycle) — begin the daily-update habit immediately, since it compounds and is the single most visible early signal of this competency, and the cycle clock is already running
- Start Guided Exercise 2 (dependency mapping) on their first real assigned task, logging into the same bundle Exercise 1 is building

**Read:** concept notes §8–9 (commitment reliability, escalation vs. anticipation) alongside starting Exercise 3.

## Month 1

**Deliver:**
- Continue Exercise 3's status/escalation habit through the full chosen cycle without prompting — this is the artifact DL-2-P2 needs, and it fails if there's an unexplained gap partway through
- Complete Exercise 1/2's bundle to ≥5 real tickets from the same delivery unit — check at the 3-week mark whether the unit will naturally produce 5+ tickets; if not, extend the window rather than padding with unrelated tickets
- Guided Exercise 4 (cost/value decision practice) on a real decision that comes up in their work, logged within the same cycle as Exercise 3
- Guided Exercise 5 (under-scoped task resizing rehearsal) once they've built enough context to attempt it meaningfully — usually week 3–4, not day 1; remember this is rehearsal for the eventual P4 live-demo, not itself badge evidence
- Start of Autonomous Project 1 or 2 (doesn't need to be finished by end of month 1, but should be underway, and should reuse the same bundle/cycle rather than starting a third one)

**Read:** remaining concept notes (§4–7, §10–12) as the relevant exercises or real situations come up, rather than all at once.

## Buddy check — 2-week check-in questions

- "Show me your status updates from the last two weeks — was there a day with no update, and if so, why?" (Use the raw channel history, not a summary — this mirrors exactly how the DL-2 rubric will be reviewed.)
- "Walk me through a ticket you sized this week — where's the sizing rationale documented, and does the timestamp show it was written before you started work?"
- "Has anything come up that you weren't sure whether to escalate yet? What did you decide, and when — show me the ticket or message, not just tell me?"
- "How many tickets are in your delivery-unit bundle so far, and are they all from the same sprint/ramp period, or has anything drifted in from elsewhere?" (Catch bundle contamination early — a mixed-source bundle will fail review later.)
- "Is there anything about how this team prioritizes or communicates that still feels unclear or arbitrary?" (This question is for catching onboarding gaps, not testing the new hire — treat the answer as feedback on onboarding quality, not a mark against them.)
- "Has a client request come in yet that felt like it was outside what was originally scoped? What did you do with it?" (Only ask if relevant to their engagement — the point is to check whether they're absorbing scope silently or flagging it.)
