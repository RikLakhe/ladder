# PF 1 — Incremental Value Delivery: Live Demo Checklist

**Used when:** technical/promotion interview or live facilitation session (40–60 min).
**Covers:** P4, P5, P6. (P7 is assessed via portfolio + teaching demonstration only — see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`.)

**Redesign note (2026-08-03):** unchanged in structure — these scenarios already require a full, realistic build (a real backlog item, a full facilitated breakdown session) rather than a checkbox quiz, per the methodology's live-demo exception in Rule 4 of `01-code-review-rubric.md`'s award logic. See `badges.md` for the badge layered on top of each scenario's pass bar.

## Scenario: P4 — Live planning scenario

**Setup:** Give the candidate an under-scoped, oversized, or wrongly-prioritized backlog item during a simulated planning session. 40 minutes.

**For the candidate:**
- You'll be handed a backlog item that's under-scoped, oversized, or mis-prioritized, and asked to resize it and correct its priority and dependency notes live — no prep material is provided in advance
- Expect a follow-up curveball partway through (e.g. a moved-up deadline, or a client-driven scope change that wasn't in the original SOW line); you'll be asked to explain what changes about your plan and what doesn't, and — for the scope-change variant — whether and how you'd route it as a change request rather than absorb it silently
- Your time: about 25 minutes actively working the scenario, plus wrap-up discussion, inside the 40-minute session

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Resizes the task appropriately for incremental delivery, unaided
- [ ] Identifies and corrects a missing or wrong dependency note
- [ ] Identifies and corrects a wrongly-prioritized backlog item, explaining the reprioritization rationale
- [ ] Makes — and documents the rationale for — a decision under incomplete information, including when the scenario is escalated to a simulated high-pressure condition (e.g. "the deadline just moved up a week")

_Editorial note: the standard's PC 1.9 ("correct a task's priority and dependency notes during planning, unaided") is a compound criterion; the two checklist items above split it for explicit, separate scoring since P4 has no portfolio backstop for this PF. This is a pipeline-level workaround, not a change to the standard — see the logged standard defect for a possible future PC split._

**Prompts:**
- "What's missing from this ticket that would block someone else from picking it up cold?"
- "The deadline just moved up a week — what changes about your plan, and what doesn't?"
- "The client just asked for this on top of what was scoped — what do you do before you start writing code?"

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item. **Time budget:** 10 min setup, 25 min scenario, 5 min wrap-up.

## Scenario: P5 — Epic breakdown facilitation

**Setup:** Candidate facilitates a mock breakdown session for an epic, with 2–3 interviewers playing team members holding mildly conflicting priority opinions. 45 minutes.

**For the candidate:**
- You'll facilitate a live breakdown of an epic for a small group playing team members with mildly conflicting priority opinions — come ready to lead, not just propose a breakdown on paper
- You'll need to surface and resolve at least one priority or urgency disagreement within the session, and guide the group through a decision made with incomplete information
- Your time: about 30 minutes facilitating, inside the 45-minute session

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Breaks the epic into well-scoped, prioritized pieces the group can restate back accurately
- [ ] Surfaces at least one point of priority/urgency misalignment and resolves it within the session
- [ ] Guides the group through at least one decision made under incomplete information (1.15)

**Prompts:**
- "How would you explain this priority order to a stakeholder who disagrees with it?"

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item. **Time budget:** 10 min setup, 30 min session, 5 min wrap-up.

## Scenario: P6 — Cross-team breakdown facilitation

**Setup:** Candidate facilitates breakdown of a cross-team initiative, with 2 interviewers representing teams with competing timelines. 50 minutes.

**For the candidate:**
- You'll facilitate a live breakdown of a cross-team initiative for two people representing teams with competing timelines — come ready to produce pieces both simulated teams can restate back as understood
- You'll need to surface a cross-team dependency conflict and either resolve it or produce an explicit plan to resolve it, and guide the group through risk or uncertainty spanning both teams' work
- Your time: about 35 minutes facilitating, inside the 50-minute session

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Breaks cross-team work into pieces both simulated teams can restate back as understood
- [ ] Surfaces a cross-team dependency conflict and either resolves it or produces an explicit plan to resolve it
- [ ] Guides the group through risk or uncertainty spanning both teams' work (1.16)

**Prompts:**
- "If Team B's timeline slips two weeks, walk me through exactly what happens to Team A's plan."

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item. **Time budget:** 10 min setup, 35 min session, 5 min wrap-up.
