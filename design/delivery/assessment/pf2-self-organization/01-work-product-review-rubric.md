# PF 2 — Self-Organization: Work Product Review Rubric

**Redesign note (2026-08-03):** previously scored on 2–3 disconnected status notes plus a single decision write-up. That's closer to a spot-check than portfolio-worthy evidence of a reliability pattern. This rubric now requires evidence spanning one **full, continuous delivery cycle (2–4 weeks)**, so the reviewer can see whether proactive communication and cost/value judgment are a sustained habit, not a one-off good week.

**Used when:** reviewing a candidate's real status/escalation record and cost/value decisions across one continuous delivery cycle.
**Covers:** P2–P4. (P5–P7 are assessed via portfolio — see `03-portfolio-requirements.md` — since roadmap/expectation-setting judgment shows better in a full example than in day-to-day status records.)

| Criteria | Does not meet P2 | Meets P2 | Meets P3 | Meets P4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proactive progress & blocker communication | Progress only surfaces when someone asks; record has gaps with no explanation | Record across the full 2–4 week cycle shows daily progress reported without being asked, and at least one committed task delivered with visible urgency (2.1, 2.2) — a single good day is not sufficient | Record shows every blocker, delay, or cost overrun in the cycle escalated the same day it was identified, without prompting (2.3) — reviewer checks dates against when the issue actually arose, not the candidate's account of when they noticed | Record shows at least one blocker, delay, or cost overrun communicated **before** it became escalation-worthy, with a clear "how did you know before it was urgent" explanation (2.4) |
| Cost/value tradeoff reasoning | Cost/value calls made without a stated rationale anywhere in the cycle, or no cost/value thinking applied at all | At least one real decision in the cycle shows the candidate asking a senior engineer for help weighing cost against value, and can walk through the reasoning afterward (2.8) | At least one real decision in the cycle is weighed against value with senior engineer input, using a named tradeoff or framework rather than gut feel, documented at the time (2.9) | At least one real decision in the cycle is weighed unaided with a documented rationale, **and** the candidate has suggested a concrete economic tradeoff to a teammate on their own work during the same cycle (2.10, 2.11) — both parts required, not either/or |

**Pass bar:** all criteria at or above "Meets [target level]" across the whole cycle's record, not a best-case day; no criterion below "Meets" for the level being assessed.

**For the candidate:**
- Bring your full status/standup/escalation record for one continuous 2–4 week delivery cycle — not a hand-picked set of good days. If you report status to a client PM or account manager in addition to your LFT lead, either record is acceptable evidence, and bring both if they diverge.
- Every blocker, delay, or cost overrun you flagged during that cycle needs to be visible in the record with dates, not recalled from memory afterward — the reviewer checks timestamps against when the issue actually started.
- Bring at least one specific cost/value decision write-up from within the same cycle (or be ready to walk through it in detail if nothing was written down at the time).
- "Done" at P3 means every escalation in the cycle — not just one — was raised the same day it was identified, without being prompted.
- "Done" at P4 means the record shows at least one issue caught and communicated before it became escalation-worthy, **and** a cost/value tradeoff you suggested to a teammate on their own work within the same cycle.
- Expect a 30-minute review folded into normal review cadence — budget time beforehand to assemble the cycle's record if it isn't already centralized in one place.

**How to run this review:**
- Time: 30 minutes
- Artifacts to collect: the full status/standup/escalation record for one continuous 2–4 week cycle, plus one cost/value decision write-up from the same cycle (or a walkthrough if nothing was written down)
- Who can assess: the candidate's manager or a peer with recent working history alongside them during that same cycle
