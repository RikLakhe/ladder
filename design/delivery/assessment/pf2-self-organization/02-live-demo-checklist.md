# PF 2 — Self-Organization: Retrospective & Cost/Value Walkthrough Checklist

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template calls this a "live demo checklist" for a hands-on technical scenario. Self-Organization's Evidence Guide assesses P2–P4 through a structured retrospective conversation, not a live build — so this instrument is a guided interview rather than a live task, occupying the same slot in the pipeline.

**Redesign note (2026-08-03):** corroboration from a manager/teammate (item 2 below) is not optional color — it is required evidence the walkthrough must produce, precisely because a self-reported reliability story is the easiest thing in this PF to overstate. See `badges.md` for the badge layered on top of this checklist's pass bar.

**Used when:** promotion review or interview (30–45 min structured conversation).
**Covers:** P2–P4. (P5–P7 are assessed via portfolio — see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`.)

**For the candidate:**
- Come ready to walk through one real delivery cycle from the last 2–4 weeks, and one specific cost/value tradeoff you made during it — pick a real example, not a hypothetical
- Be ready to speak to specific dates and examples of how you communicated progress and blockers, not general impressions
- If you're being assessed at P4, bring a concrete case of a blocker you caught and flagged before it needed escalation, and a cost/value tradeoff you suggested to a teammate on their own work
- Your manager or teammates may be asked beforehand to corroborate your track record on commitments — this isn't a surprise, just make sure the examples you bring are ones others would recognize
- Your time: the session runs 30–45 minutes total, with roughly 25–35 minutes on the walkthrough itself

## Session: Delivery retrospective + cost/value walkthrough

**Setup:** Walk through a recent 2–4 week delivery cycle and one specific cost/value decision the candidate made. 30–45 minutes.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Describes communicating progress and blockers proactively, with specific dates/examples, not generalities
- [ ] Track record (corroborated by manager or teammates beforehand) shows commitments met or visibly renegotiated, not silently missed
- [ ] Describes a real cost/value tradeoff using an explicit framework or reasoning, not just "it felt right"
- [ ] **P4 only:** describes a blocker caught and communicated before it needed escalation, and an economic tradeoff suggested to a teammate unaided

**Prompts (level-differentiated):**
- P2: "Walk me through a task you delivered recently — how did you keep the team posted along the way?"
- P3: "Tell me about a blocker you escalated. How did you decide it was worth raising, and when?"
- P4: "Describe a blocker you caught early — what tipped you off before it became urgent?" / "Describe a cost/value call you made without help — what was the tradeoff, and what did you decide?"
- If on a client engagement: "Was there a time the client's own process (their ceremonies, tooling, or reporting cadence) constrained how you'd normally have handled a blocker or status update? How did you stay proactive within that constraint?"

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item; overall recommendation.
**Time budget:** 5 min intro, 25–35 min walkthrough, 5 min wrap-up.
