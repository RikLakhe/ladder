# PF 5 — AI-Assisted Engineering: Portfolio Requirements

**Used when:** promotion review to P5/P6/P7, or senior/staff/architect-level hiring.

**For the candidate:** every item must be real, adopted work with evidence, not a written-and-forgotten standard — the "good" column is the bar a badge (below) actually requires.

## Required evidence items

| Item | Maps to PC | Quality bar: sufficient | Quality bar: good |
|---|---|---|---|
| A team-level AI tool-use/prompting standard they established | 5.4 [P5] | Standard is documented | Standard is visibly enforced in code review (cite examples), not just written and forgotten |
| (P6+) Evidence of driving AI workflow adoption/quality across several teams | 5.5 [P6] | Adoption work documented for ≥2 teams | Adoption evidenced per `../badges.md` §1.6 (a named contact at each team + a dated reference + one line on what changed) with a specific team-level gap identified and closed with team leads, plus before/after evidence |
| (P7) An organizational AI workflow standard they set and govern | 5.6 [P7] | Standard is documented and published | Standard includes an outcome metric (not a usage metric) and evidence it's being tracked |
| A client-facing AI tool-use policy they applied or helped define (any level P5+) | 5.4–5.6 | Documents which AI tools are approved per client/contract, what must never be pasted into them, and disclosure requirements | Policy has been applied across multiple client engagements with differing restrictions, and includes how multi-client hygiene (no cross-client context leakage) is enforced in practice |

## Submission format

- Link to the standard document, adoption plan, or governance document
- 1-paragraph context: what workflow problem existed, what changed, how it's measured
- For P7: explicitly state the outcome metric used and why it was chosen over a usage metric
- **Client confidentiality:** if the standard/evidence references a specific client's restrictions or codebase, submit a sanitized/anonymized version (or a client-approved walkthrough) — do not submit literal client code, contract text, or data as evidence. **Expediting sanitization/export sign-off is the sponsoring delivery lead/account manager's job, not the candidate's to chase alone**; where extraction is contractually impossible, use the in-place attestation path in `../badges.md` §1.5
- Time spent assembling this submission counts as normal professional development time, coordinated with your delivery/account lead

## Review process

- Reviewed by a P6+ engineer not on the candidate's team (technical verifier), plus the candidate's manager. **For the P6 item specifically, "manager" means the candidate's delivery/account manager, co-signing to confirm the cross-team adoption claimed actually happened** — see `../badges.md` §1.1
- 30-minute review session: reviewer reads beforehand, session is for probing questions
- Questions to ask: "How do you know teams actually follow this, versus just agreeing to it in a meeting?" "What's a usage metric you deliberately avoided using as your success measure, and why?"
- **P7 only:** add a teaching demonstration — candidate teaches the organizational AI workflow standard (including the outcome-metric rationale) to a group; reviewer observes for clarity and evidence of adoption beyond the candidate's own team

## Badges

- P5 item → **TS-5-P5 (Team AI Standard Setter)**
- P6 item → **TS-5-P6 (Cross-Team AI Adoption Driver)**
- P7 item + teaching demonstration → **TS-5-P7 (Organizational AI Workflow Governor)**

See `../badges.md`. Record the award (Badge ID, date, artifact link, verifier) against the candidate's file per `../badges.md` Part 3.
