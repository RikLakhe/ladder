# PF 2 — Communication: Work Product Review Rubric

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template names this file `01-code-review-rubric.md` for a code-artifact review. This PF's work products are written/verbal communication samples and documentation, so this instrument is retitled — same slot in the pipeline, same pass-bar logic.

**Redesign note (2026-08-03):** this rubric is now run against a **4–6 week dated evidence bundle**, not a hand-picked "1–2 samples" set — see `../00-evidence-bundle-standard.md`. Clearing a row also feeds a badge in `../badges.md` (`FCC-2-P2`/`P3`/`P4`); the badge's "Evidence required" field points back to the exact rows below.

**Used when:** reviewing real communication samples and documentation contributions.
**Covers:** P2–P4. From P5 up, this PF is assessed via portfolio and an observed team/cross-team communication (see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`).

| Criteria | Does not meet P2 | Meets P2 | Meets P3 | Meets P4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Audience-oriented communication | Communication is unclear, or the same regardless of who's reading/listening | Bundle includes ≥1 written or verbal sample that is clear, concise, and shows awareness of the audience; candidate can describe how they listened and responded to nonverbal cues in a real conversation | Bundle sample extends to a non-technical subject or audience, described as "usually" reliable — occasional misses are acceptable at this level | Bundle shows consistently clear, audience-appropriate communication across both technical and non-technical subjects, drawn from ≥2 dated instances across the window — misses are the exception, not expected |
| Knowledge sharing & documentation | Knowledge stays undocumented and only shared verbally, inconsistently | Bundle includes ≥1 dated instance of knowledge shared with teammates, even if informally | Bundle includes ≥1 dated, documented contribution to team documentation, plus a described instance of proactively spotting a knowledge-sharing opportunity | Bundle includes a dated documentation contribution, and a described instance of encouraging a teammate to share knowledge or document something |
| Client-facing & cross-timezone communication | Client-facing sample (if any) is tonally miscalibrated (over-casual, over-hedged, or unexplained jargon) for an external enterprise audience, or candidate has no async handoff note in the bundle and cannot describe how they hand off work across a timezone gap, and the engagement does have a cross-timezone counterpart | Bundle includes ≥1 dated async written handoff note (status update or task handoff) that let a teammate or client contact in a different time zone act on it without needing a same-day reply — internal-teammate-facing is acceptable at this level, a client-facing counterpart is not required yet. **N/A per `../badges.md` §1.7** if the candidate's engagement genuinely has no cross-timezone counterpart (fully co-located team, single-timezone client) — a valid non-failing state, not a gap to manufacture evidence for | Bundle includes ≥1 client-facing artifact (status update, email, or ticket comment to a client stakeholder) showing calibrated tone and no internal-only jargon, in addition to the P2 async-handoff bar carried forward (or its N/A state, if still applicable); can describe a written async handoff that let a US-based counterpart pick up work without a live call | Bundle shows ≥2 dated client-ready written updates (status report, escalation note, or steering-committee-bound summary) produced without a hand-holding rewrite from their lead; routinely hands off open items async across the Nepal/India–US gap with enough context that the receiving side doesn't need to wait for a live sync |

**Pass bar:** all criteria at or above "Meets [target level]"; no criterion below "Meets" for the level being assessed; bundle instances are dated within the stated 4–6 week window per `../00-evidence-bundle-standard.md`.

**For the candidate:**
- Assemble a dated bundle covering a real 4–6 week working window — the count specified per level in the table above (a written doc, a design write-up, a Slack thread explaining something technical) and a technical explanation you've given to a non-technical audience, even informally
- Include any documentation contribution you've made to team docs, dated
- Include ≥1 dated async written handoff note (status update or task handoff) written for a teammate or client contact in a different time zone — this is a required part of the P2 bundle if your engagement has a cross-timezone counterpart at all; if it genuinely doesn't (fully co-located team, single-timezone client), say so and this item is N/A, not a gap
- At P3+, if any of your work involves a client stakeholder, also include one client-facing artifact (status update, email, ticket comment)
- Have this bundle ready before the review session; the rubric assesses real work, not a specially-prepared showcase
- "Done" at P2 means your bundle shows clear, audience-aware communication, an async cross-timezone handoff note (or its valid N/A state), and you can describe listening/responding to cues in a real conversation; "done" at P3 means that extends to a non-technical subject or audience, reliably "usually", plus a calibrated client-facing artifact where the candidate's role includes client contact; "done" at P4 means it's consistent across both technical and non-technical subjects (≥2 dated instances), with a documented team-documentation contribution, an instance of encouraging a teammate to share knowledge or document something, and ≥2 dated client-ready communications produced without a rewrite from their lead
- Clearing this rubric at your target level is the evidence behind badge `FCC-2-P2`/`P3`/`P4` in `../badges.md`

**How to run this review:**
- Time: 20–25 minutes
- Artifacts to collect: the candidate's dated bundle — the count specified per level in the table above, any documentation contributions, an async cross-timezone handoff note (required from P2 where a cross-timezone counterpart exists), and — where applicable to the candidate's engagement — a client-facing update/email
- Who can assess: the candidate's manager or any P4+ engineer familiar with their written work; where available, input from the client-facing lead (delivery/account lead or PM) on the calibration of client-facing samples
- **Note on evidence availability:** do not require a client-facing artifact from candidates whose current project has no direct client contact — score that row as not applicable (`../badges.md` §1.7) and weight the remaining criteria accordingly, rather than failing the candidate for lack of access. Likewise, the async cross-timezone handoff note is N/A per §1.7 if the candidate's engagement genuinely has no cross-timezone counterpart (fully co-located team or client) — it is not waived merely because the candidate has no *direct* client contact, since it applies equally to a same-company teammate in a different time zone; it is only N/A when no cross-timezone counterpart of any kind exists.
- **On AM-mediated client contact (P3 "direct client communication"):** where a candidate's client-facing artifact was drafted by the candidate but reviewed and approved by the account/delivery manager before being sent to the client, this still counts as valid client-facing evidence for this row — the calibration/tone skill being assessed lives in the drafting, not in who clicked "send." This is distinct from an artifact the candidate never touched (e.g., a template the AM wrote entirely and the candidate only delivered) — the latter does not satisfy this row.
- If a bundle instance is a client-confidential artifact that can't be exported, apply `../badges.md` §1.5 (in-place attestation); if the candidate lost access to a client's systems mid-window before extracting an instance, apply §1.5a
