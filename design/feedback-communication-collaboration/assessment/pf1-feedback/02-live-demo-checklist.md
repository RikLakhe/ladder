# PF 1 — Feedback: Structured Conversation Checklist

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template calls this a "live demo checklist" for a hands-on technical scenario. This PF's Evidence Guide assesses P2–P4 through a structured conversation, not a live build — so this instrument is a guided interview occupying the same slot in the pipeline.

**Redesign note (2026-08-03):** this conversation now cross-checks the candidate's dated evidence bundle (`../00-evidence-bundle-standard.md`), assembled and brought by the candidate per `01-work-product-review-rubric.md`, rather than relying on recalled examples alone. A pass here plus the rubric bar is the evidence for badge `FCC-1-P2`/`P3`/`P4` in `../badges.md`.

**Used when:** promotion review or interview (25–35 min structured conversation).
**Covers:** P2–P4. (P5–P7 are assessed via portfolio and a manager/peer observation — see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`.)

## Session: Feedback exchange conversation

**Setup:** A conversation covering feedback the candidate has given and sought recently, cross-checked against their dated evidence bundle. 25–35 minutes.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Can explain what makes feedback useful (specific, tied to a concrete instance, actionable) — P2 baseline
- [ ] Describes a specific, real, dated instance from the bundle of giving feedback, not a generic description of "how I give feedback"
- [ ] Describes a specific, real, dated instance from the bundle of seeking feedback and what they did differently afterward
- [ ] **P4 only:** describes a bundle instance of feedback given to someone outside their immediate team, and how they adapted the framing or delivery for that audience — if the engagement is staff-aug or fixed-bid, this may be a client lead or client PM rather than an internal stakeholder
- [ ] **P4 only:** where the candidate works in a matrixed reporting structure (client stakeholder directs day-to-day, LFT lead holds the formal record), describes how they sought/reconciled feedback from both, and how they made sure client-side feedback reached their LFT manager to count toward evaluation

**Prompts (level-differentiated):**
- P2: "What's the difference between feedback that's useful and feedback that isn't? Walk me through the feedback-seeking instance in your bundle."
- P3: "Walk me through one of the feedback-given instances in your bundle — what made it land well?"
- P4: "Tell me about the instance in your bundle where you gave feedback to someone outside engineering — a business stakeholder, which may be a client lead or PM you work with day-to-day. How did you adjust it for them?"
- P4 (matrixed context): "If a client stakeholder gives you feedback that's different from what your LFT manager sees or hears, how do you make sure both are accounted for?"

**For the candidate:**
- Bring your dated 4–6 week evidence bundle (assembled per `01-work-product-review-rubric.md` and `../00-evidence-bundle-standard.md`) — the conversation walks through specific, dated instances from it, not a general description of "how I give feedback"
- Real instances can include a client status update, an escalation email, or an async cross-timezone handoff, not only internal PR comments or 1:1s
- At P4, also be ready to describe feedback you gave to someone outside your immediate team, and how you adapted it for that audience — for many engineers this is a client-side lead or PM, not an internal LFT stakeholder
- If you work in a matrixed setup (client directs your day-to-day, an LFT lead owns your formal feedback record), be ready to describe how you've navigated getting feedback from both, and how client-side feedback made it back to your LFT manager
- Time budget: 25–35 minutes total (5 min intro, 20–25 min conversation, 5 min wrap-up) — no advance materials required beyond your assembled bundle

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item; overall recommendation.
