# PF 1 — Feedback: Work Product Review Rubric

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template names this file `01-code-review-rubric.md` for a code-artifact review. This PF's work products are feedback exchanges (PR comments, 1:1 notes, written feedback), so this instrument is retitled — same slot in the pipeline, same pass-bar logic.

**Redesign note (2026-08-03):** this rubric is now run against a **4–6 week dated evidence bundle**, not a hand-picked "2–3 examples" set — see `../00-evidence-bundle-standard.md`. Clearing a row also feeds a badge in `../badges.md` (`FCC-1-P2`/`P3`/`P4`); the badge's "Evidence required" field points back to the exact rows below.

**Used when:** reviewing real, documented feedback a candidate has given or sought.
**Covers:** P2–P4. From P5 up, this PF is assessed via portfolio and a manager/peer observation (see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`) — fostering a feedback culture shows better in a fuller example than in a single exchange.

| Criteria | Does not meet P2 | Meets P2 | Meets P3 | Meets P4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Giving feedback usefully | Cannot describe what makes feedback useful, or gives only vague praise/criticism ("good job," "this is wrong") | Explains what makes feedback useful when asked, even without much of a track record giving it yet | Bundle contains ≥2 dated, documented, specific feedback instances (tied to a concrete instance) given to a teammate or their LFT lead/manager over the 4–6 week window | Bundle contains ≥1 dated instance of feedback given to someone outside their immediate team — a business stakeholder, which on a staff-aug or fixed-bid engagement may be a client lead or client PM rather than an internal LFT stakeholder — adapted for that audience |
| Seeking and using feedback | Never asks for feedback, or asks but shows no evidence of acting on it | Bundle contains ≥1 dated instance of asking for feedback and describing what they changed as a result | Same bar as P2 — this is a day-one expectation carried through P4 per the LFT matrix's combined P2–P4 row | Same bar as P2 — carried forward; growth here is expected to show as habit and range across the bundle window, not a new tier of behavior |

**Pass bar:** all criteria at or above "Meets [target level]"; no criterion below "Meets" for the level being assessed; bundle instances are dated within the stated 4–6 week window (or the most recent available window, per `00-evidence-bundle-standard.md`'s exceptions). The Seeking & Receiving Feedback row does not gain a new tier at P3/P4 — don't penalize a candidate for "not growing" on this row between P2 and P4; check instead that the habit has continued across the window.

**For the candidate:**
- Assemble a dated bundle covering a real 4–6 week working window — 2–3 real, documented feedback instances you've given recently (PR/code review comments, 1:1 notes, written peer feedback, a client status update or escalation email, or an async cross-timezone handoff note where you gave feedback on someone else's work) plus 1 instance of you seeking feedback and what you changed as a result — not specially-constructed examples, your actual work, logged with dates as you go rather than reconstructed the night before
- If you work on a staff-aug or fixed-bid engagement where day-to-day direction comes from a client lead or PM rather than your LFT manager, at least one instance can (and for P4, should) be feedback given to or received from that client-side stakeholder — this counts as much as an internal exchange
- Have this bundle ready before the review session; there's no separate prep ceremony beyond gathering and dating the artifacts
- "Done" at P2 means you can explain what makes feedback useful even without much of a track record giving it yet, plus one dated feedback-seeking instance; "done" at P3 means your bundle has ≥2 dated, specific instances of giving useful feedback to a teammate or your LFT lead/manager; "done" at P4 means at least one bundle instance extends to someone outside your immediate team — often a client stakeholder in a matrixed engagement — adapted for that audience
- Clearing this rubric at your target level is the evidence behind badge `FCC-1-P2`/`P3`/`P4` in `../badges.md` — ask your assessor whether the badge sign-off happens in the same session

**How to run this review:**
- Time: 20–25 minutes (slightly longer than the prior 15–20 to review a dated bundle rather than recalled examples)
- Artifacts to collect: the candidate's dated 4–6 week evidence bundle per `00-evidence-bundle-standard.md` (2–3 feedback-given instances + 1 feedback-sought instance minimum)
- Who can assess: the candidate's LFT manager, any P4+ engineer who has exchanged feedback with them recently, or — where relevant to the engagement — input from a client lead/PM the candidate works with day-to-day, gathered by the assessor rather than requiring the client's direct participation in the session
- If a bundle instance is a client-confidential artifact that can't be exported, apply `../badges.md` §1.5 (in-place attestation); if the candidate lost access to a client's systems mid-window before extracting an instance, apply §1.5a
