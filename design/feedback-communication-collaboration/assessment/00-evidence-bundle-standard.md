# Feedback, Communication & Collaboration — Evidence Bundle Standard

**Status:** active — redesign layer, 2026-08-03. Applies to all three PFs' Phase D instruments (`pf1-feedback/`, `pf2-communication/`, `pf3-collaboration/`) and to the badge catalog (`badges.md`).

## Why this exists

The original Phase D build (V1, 2026-07-06) assessed P2–P4 primarily through a structured conversation cross-checked against "2–3 real instances." That is enough to confirm a candidate can *describe* the behavior, but it is close to a checkbox exercise: a candidate can pass by recalling favorable anecdotes, and an assessor has no way to see the behavior's actual density, recency, or range across a real working period. This standard tightens the evidence bar at every level to require an assembled bundle of dated, real work product — the same "substantial, portfolio-worthy work" discipline this pipeline already uses for P5–P7 — while keeping the assessment modes (structured conversation, live demo, portfolio) the standard's Evidence Guide already specifies.

## The bundle requirement (replaces "bring 2–3 examples")

For every PF, at every level P2–P4, the candidate assembles a **dated evidence bundle spanning a real 4–6 week working window** (not cherry-picked from their whole career), containing:

1. **The count specified in that PF's rubric** (e.g. PF1 P3 = 2 documented feedback instances) — but drawn from that window, with dates, not "whichever 2 examples are strongest."
2. **At least one instance involving someone outside the candidate's closest working pair** (a different teammate, a client stakeholder, a cross-timezone counterpart) where the standard's scope calls for a widening audience at that level — this prevents a bundle built entirely from one comfortable relationship.
3. **A one-line dated log entry per instance** (what happened, when, who was involved, real artifact link/reference) — assembled by the candidate as they go, not reconstructed from memory the night before the review. This log itself becomes part of the reviewable evidence.

The structured conversation / live demo checklist is still how the assessor probes and cross-checks the bundle (per each PF's instrument) — the bundle changes what's on the table, not the session format.

## Where the bundle requirement doesn't fit as-is

- **On-call / rostering-dependent rows (PF3):** if the candidate genuinely wasn't rostered in the 4–6 week window, the bundle substitutes the most recent rostered window available, or is marked N/A per the existing non-rostered exception in `pf3-collaboration/01-work-product-review-rubric.md`.
- **Client-confidential artifacts:** apply §1.5 of `badges.md` (redact, or in-place attestation) rather than excluding the instance from the bundle.
- **Access lost to a rotated-off client mid-window:** apply §1.5a of `badges.md` — the partial bundle is preserved and counted, not discarded, and a badge tied to this evidence goes to "blocked — assignment-limited" (§1.7) rather than "not earned" if the window couldn't be completed.

## P5–P7

No change to bundle mechanics — these levels are already assessed via portfolio + observation/teaching demonstration per each PF's `03-portfolio-requirements.md`, which already requires dated, real evidence with before/after description. This standard's tightening is specifically aimed at closing the P2–P4 gap.

## Relationship to badges

Each badge in `badges.md` names, in its "Evidence required" field, the exact bundle/portfolio item it draws on. A badge cannot be awarded from a conversation alone at any level — the bundle (P2–P4) or portfolio item (P5–P7) is the artifact of record.
