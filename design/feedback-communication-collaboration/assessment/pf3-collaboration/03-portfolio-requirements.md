# PF 3 — Collaboration: Portfolio Requirements

**Redesign note (2026-08-03):** each row below is also the named evidence source for a P5/P6/P7 badge in `../badges.md` (`FCC-3-P5`, `FCC-3-P6`, `FCC-3-P7`). A badge is not awarded from this table alone — the reviewer sign-off and, for culture/adoption rows, the adoption-evidence artifact (`../badges.md` §1.6) are also required.

**Used when:** promotion review to P5/P6/P7, or lead/principal/architect-level hiring.

## Submission format

- Link to any documentation of the initiative, norm, or relationship outcome
- 1-paragraph context: the situation, who was involved (naming whether counterparts are LFT-internal or client-side), and how the outcome is known (not just asserted)
- For the coaching-on-client-structure/multi-engagement row: name the teammate coached and how the coaching happened (a specific conversation, a paired session, a written note) — a description of the candidate's own client-structure adaptation, without a named teammate on the receiving end, does not satisfy this row; if no coachable teammate exists in the candidate's current staffing, state that explicitly and mark the row N/A per `../badges.md` §1.7 instead
- For P6/P7: name the teams/individuals involved (including client-side counterparts and, where relevant, which client engagements) and any friction encountered; adoption claims must include the `../badges.md` §1.6 3-part artifact (named contact, dated reference, what changed)

## Review process

- Reviewed by a P6+ engineer not on the candidate's team, plus the candidate's manager
- 30-minute review session
- Questions to ask: "Who would push back on this story, and what would they say?" "What's an example of a relationship or disagreement you handled badly, and what did you learn?" "If a client's engineering leadership reviewed this evidence, would it hold up, or does it read as internally-facing only?" "Who did you coach on client-structure or multi-engagement collaboration, and what did they do differently afterward?" (or, if N/A: "if you had a junior teammate on this account, what would you tell them about working inside this client's structure?")
- For candidates supporting more than one concurrent engagement: ask for at least one example spanning two engagements, not just the strongest single-engagement story
- **P7 only:** add a teaching/culture demonstration — candidate presents how they've handled organization-wide disagreement or relationship-building to a group outside their usual circle
- Where the row also serves as badge evidence (`FCC-3-P5`/`P6`/`P7`), the reviewer's sign-off and date, plus (at P6) the delivery/account manager co-signer per `../badges.md` §1.1, are recorded against the badge as well as the portfolio record
