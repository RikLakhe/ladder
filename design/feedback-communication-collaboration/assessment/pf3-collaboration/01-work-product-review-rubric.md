# PF 3 — Collaboration: Work Product Review Rubric

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template names this file `01-code-review-rubric.md` for a code-artifact review. This PF's work products are collaboration records (on-call logs, peer feedback, retro notes), so this instrument is retitled — same slot in the pipeline, same pass-bar logic.

**Redesign note (2026-08-03):** this rubric is now run against a **4–6 week dated evidence bundle**, not a hand-picked example set — see `../00-evidence-bundle-standard.md`. Clearing a row also feeds a badge in `../badges.md` (`FCC-3-P2`/`P3`/`P4`); the badge's "Evidence required" field points back to the exact rows below.

**Used when:** reviewing real, documented collaboration instances.
**Covers:** P2–P4. From P5 up, this PF is assessed via portfolio and a peer/manager observation (see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`).

| Criteria | Does not meet P2 | Meets P2 | Meets P3 | Meets P4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Helping teammates (core) | Does not help when asked, or help is inconsistent enough that teammates stop asking | Bundle includes ≥1 dated instance of helping when requested | Bundle includes ≥1 dated instance of helping resolve an obstacle, blocker, or task when requested — teammate may be LFT or client-side (e.g., unblocking a client engineer on a shared story) | Bundle includes ≥1 dated instance of helping proactively, without being asked first |
| Credit-sharing | Does not give or share credit for shared work | Bundle includes ≥1 dated instance of giving or sharing credit for shared work | Same bar as P2 — carried forward; this is a day-one expectation and doesn't gain a new tier at P3/P4 | Same bar as P2 — carried forward |
| On-call support | N/A — on-call is not a P2 expectation | N/A — on-call is not a P2 expectation | On-call record from the bundle window showing scheduled support provided, **or** documented as not yet rostered for on-call (as determined by Engineering Management) — non-rostered status is a valid, non-failing state (`../badges.md` §1.7) | Same bar as P3 — carried forward (on-call is a day-one expectation through P5 per the standard); non-rostered status remains a valid non-failing state |
| Relationship building | No evidence of relationships beyond required daily interaction | Bundle includes ≥1 dated relationship example with a teammate or manager (LFT or client-side, if embedded in a client team) | Bundle includes ≥1 dated relationship example with their product counterpart — on a client engagement, this may be a client-side PM or product owner rather than an internal counterpart | Bundle includes ≥1 dated relationship example with a business stakeholder relevant to the team — for engagement-based work, this includes a client-side stakeholder, not only an internal one |
| Working within the engagement's structure | Not assessed at "does not meet" tier | Not a P2 expectation | Bundle includes ≥1 dated instance of adapting to a client's existing tools, ceremonies, or conventions (e.g., client's Jira workflow, client's code review norms) rather than defaulting to LFT's own — applies when staffed on a client engagement; N/A if not yet client-facing (`../badges.md` §1.7) | Same bar as P3 — carried forward; N/A if not yet client-facing |
| Multi-engagement boundary-keeping | Not assessed at "does not meet" tier | N/A — not a P2 expectation | N/A — not a P3 expectation | Bundle includes ≥2 dated instances, across distinct engagements or time periods, of keeping context and priorities separated while staffed on ≥2 concurrent client engagements — without leaking one client's information, data, or priorities into another's; a single instance is not sufficient to demonstrate this as a sustained habit rather than a one-off — applies only when staffed on ≥2 concurrent engagements; N/A per `../badges.md` §1.7 if the candidate is single-staffed |
| Handling disagreement | Avoids disagreement entirely, or becomes defensive when challenged | Bundle includes ≥1 dated instance each of: sharing an opinion respectfully, changing their mind based on someone else's input, and working with a teammate to resolve a disagreement in a healthy manner | Bundle includes ≥1 dated instance of approaching a real disagreement non-defensively, with a specific example of using a contradictory opinion productively | Bundle includes ≥1 dated instance of encouraging a quieter teammate to share an opinion in a real discussion |

**Pass bar:** all criteria at or above "Meets [target level]"; no criterion below "Meets" for the level being assessed; bundle instances are dated within the stated 4–6 week window per `../00-evidence-bundle-standard.md`. On-call support is scored independently from the core helping and credit-sharing criteria: a P3 or P4 candidate who has never been rostered for on-call (as determined by Engineering Management, not the candidate) is not penalized for it — "N/A — not yet rostered for on-call" satisfies this criterion. Likewise, "working within the engagement's structure" and "multi-engagement boundary-keeping" are N/A (not failing) for a candidate who has only worked on internal, fully-LFT-staffed, or single-client-staffed initiatives so far.

**For the candidate:**
- Assemble a dated bundle covering a real 4–6 week working window: on-call records if applicable, any peer feedback that mentions your collaboration, retro notes describing a disagreement you were part of, and dated instances of helping, credit-sharing, and relationship-building
- If you haven't been rostered for on-call yet, that's fine — say so; it's an explicitly valid, non-failing state on that criterion, not something held against you
- Also include a specific, dated example of resolving a disagreement with a teammate in a healthy way, separate from an example of changing your own mind based on someone else's input
- If you're staffed on a client engagement, include an example of adapting to the client's tools/conventions or working alongside a client-side engineer or PM — this counts as evidence just as much as internal-only examples
- At P4, if you're staffed on ≥2 concurrent client engagements, include ≥2 dated instances, from distinct engagements or time periods, of keeping each engagement's context/priorities separate rather than letting one leak into another — one instance is not enough to show this is a sustained habit; if you're single-staffed, this criterion is N/A, not a gap
- Have this bundle ready before the review session
- "Done" at P2 means your bundle shows helping when asked, sharing credit, sharing opinions respectfully, a time you changed your mind, and a time you worked through a disagreement with a teammate; "done" at P3 means you've helped resolve a blocker when asked and provided on-call support as scheduled (or aren't yet rostered); "done" at P4 means you have a documented instance of helping proactively, without being asked, and — if multi-staffed — ≥2 documented boundary-keeping instances across distinct concurrent-engagement periods
- Clearing this rubric at your target level is the evidence behind badge `FCC-3-P2`/`P3`/`P4` in `../badges.md`

**How to run this review:**
- Time: 20–25 minutes
- Artifacts to collect: the candidate's dated bundle — on-call records if applicable, any peer feedback mentioning collaboration, retro notes describing a disagreement, and — where applicable — evidence of working within a client's ceremonies/tools/conventions or, for multi-staffed candidates, ≥2 dated instances of separating context across concurrent engagements
- Who can assess: the candidate's manager or a peer with recent working history alongside them; for embedded client-team roles, a client-facing lead who can corroborate the working relationship is also acceptable
- If a bundle instance is a client-confidential artifact that can't be exported, apply `../badges.md` §1.5 (in-place attestation); if the candidate lost access to a client's systems mid-window before extracting an instance, apply §1.5a
