# PF 3 — Collaboration: Structured Conversation Checklist

**Adaptation note:** the pipeline template calls this a "live demo checklist" for a hands-on technical scenario. This PF's Evidence Guide assesses P2–P4 through a structured conversation, not a live build — so this instrument is a guided interview occupying the same slot in the pipeline.

**Redesign note (2026-08-03):** this conversation now cross-checks the candidate's dated evidence bundle (`../00-evidence-bundle-standard.md`, assembled per `01-work-product-review-rubric.md`) rather than relying on recalled examples alone. A pass here plus the rubric bar is the evidence for badge `FCC-3-P2`/`P3`/`P4` in `../badges.md`.

**Used when:** promotion review or interview (30–40 min structured conversation).
**Covers:** P2–P4. (P5–P7 are assessed via portfolio + a peer/manager observation — see `03-portfolio-requirements.md`.)

## Session: Collaboration conversation

**Setup:** A conversation covering a time the candidate helped a teammate, a relationship they've built, and a disagreement they navigated — walked through against their dated evidence bundle. 30–40 minutes.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Describes a specific, real, dated bundle instance of helping a teammate, not a general willingness to help — teammate may be LFT or client-side (e.g., unblocking a client engineer, supporting a joint LFT-client ceremony)
- [ ] Describes a specific, dated relationship they've built and how, appropriate to their level's scope (teammate/manager at P2, product counterpart at P3, business stakeholder at P4) — if staffed on a client engagement, this may be a client-side engineer, PM, or product owner rather than an internal one
- [ ] **P2:** describes a specific, real, dated instance of working with a teammate to resolve a disagreement in a healthy manner, distinct from a general conflict-resolution philosophy
- [ ] Describes a specific, dated disagreement, how they approached it, and what happened — not a general conflict-resolution philosophy
- [ ] **P4 only:** describes proactively helping without being asked, and encouraging a quieter teammate to share an opinion, from a dated bundle instance
- [ ] **If client-facing:** describes a concrete, dated instance of adapting to the client's existing team structure, tools, or conventions (their ceremonies, their tooling, their code/process norms) rather than defaulting to LFT's own — N/A, not failing, for candidates who haven't yet worked on a client engagement

**Prompts (level-differentiated):**
- P2: "Walk me through the helping instance in your bundle — what did you do? And the instance where someone's input changed your mind about something. Also walk me through the disagreement you worked through with a teammate."
- P3: "Walk me through the disagreement instance in your bundle. How did you approach it?"
- P4: "Walk me through the instance where you helped someone without being asked. What made you notice they needed it?"
- All levels (if client-facing): "Walk me through the instance in your bundle where you had to work within a client's existing team, tools, or process rather than the way LFT normally does it. What did you adapt, and how?"

**For the candidate:**
- Bring your dated 4–6 week evidence bundle (assembled per `01-work-product-review-rubric.md`) — the conversation covers three things from it: a specific time you helped a teammate, a specific relationship you've built (scoped to your level — teammate/manager at P2, product counterpart at P3, business stakeholder at P4), and a specific disagreement you navigated
- At P2, come with two disagreement-related instances from your bundle: a time someone's input changed your mind, and a separate time you worked through a disagreement with a teammate in a healthy way
- Come with real, specific, dated instances for each — not general descriptions of your approach or philosophy
- At P4, also be ready to describe a time you helped without being asked, and a time you encouraged a quieter teammate to share an opinion
- If you're staffed on a client engagement, be ready to describe working alongside client-side engineers or PMs and adapting to their tools/conventions — this is valid evidence, not a substitute requirement
- Time budget: 30–40 minutes total (5 min intro, 25–30 min conversation, 5 min wrap-up)

**Scoring:** pass/follow-up/fail per item; overall recommendation.
**Time budget:** 5 min intro, 25–30 min conversation, 5 min wrap-up.
